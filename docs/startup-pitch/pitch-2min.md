# 2-Minute Verbal Pitch: NomNom
*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 26 July 2026*

**Word count:** ~290 · ~2 minutes at conversational pace

---

## Script

NomNom helps people in Lisbon decide where to eat from spots that creators and friends they trust actually saved — not anonymous Google stars. Lists, profiles, and Roulette open from a public link — no app install required. Picture a group stuck on “onde jantar?” — one list link, three trusted options, maybe a Roulette spin, done.

Here’s what most people miss: this category doesn’t die from missing features. DoorDash’s Zesty had unlimited budget and was gone in months. Beli, TheFork, and Uber Eats already ship creator curation. What they don’t own is the auth-free list people can open together — or the walk-in places delivery apps ignore.

So we built the artefact people can actually use: trusted lists that open without an install. Product is live — maps, lists, creator pages, Roulette — shipping for about four months. We’re pre-revenue on purpose: Year 1 is Lisbon density and proof people share and maybe pay for Snapshots or marked sponsorships — not a fantasy consumer subscription.

Portugal’s platform pool is measured in tens of millions, not tens of billions. We’re raising **two hundred thousand euros** pre-seed for eighteen months: seed about forty creators in residential Lisbon, nail organic share, prove one paid path — or kill it cleanly.

We’re not asking you to fund a Portuguese Beli. We’re asking you to fund the decide moment in one city — with the product already built.

## Delivery notes
- **Pace:** Conversational; breathe after “done” and after “ignore.”
- **Emphasis:** “without an install” · “distribution” · “one city”
- **Avoid:** Fake save stats · “no competition” · AI-as-identity · inventing creator lists · making a chat app the product
- **If interrupted:** Let them talk — especially on Uber/TheFork; concede, then restate auth-free lists + walk-ins
