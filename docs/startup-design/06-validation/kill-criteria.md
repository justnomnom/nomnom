# Kill / Pivot Criteria — NomNom

**Phase:** 8 — Validation  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Purpose:** Protect against sunk-cost on a built product. “We’ve already coded it” is not a reason to continue.

---

## Stop or hard-pivot if any of these hit

1. **Interview kill** — After 8 Lisbon diner interviews, fewer than 4 clearly confirm decide/save pain *unprompted*, **and** zero behaviour signals (paid apps, elaborate workarounds, active search). → **Stop consumer thesis** or re-problem-find.

2. **WTP kill** — Among interviewees + fake-door: fewer than 2/8 would pay any price for a local list **and** buy CTR &lt;1% on ≥200 list views **and** zero restaurant sponsor interest after 10 conversations. → **Kill Snapshot/sub**; only continue if a non-consumer model is explicit.

3. **Share-loop kill** — After 50 instrumented public list shares with polished OG: opens/share &lt;1.0. → **Distribution thesis failed**; do not recruit 30 creators on a dead loop.

4. **Creator-supply kill** — After 30 pre-built DMs over 4 weeks: claim rate &lt;5% **or** zero Story shares. → **Supply channel dead** vs Uber/TheFork exposure; freeze marketplace work.

5. **Incumbent substitution kill** — ≥5/8 interviewees say their decide problem is already solved by TheFork Feed or Uber Top Eats *and* they wouldn’t switch for a link. → **Yellow→Red**; pivot to walk-in/niche or stop.

6. **Unit-economics kill** — Lawyer confirms creator payout after VAT+store+platform &lt; €1 on a €5 Snapshot at planned take rate, and web-checkout workaround is blocked. → **Don’t launch creator paid lists** until model changes.

7. **Time-box kill** — 90 days from gate with no pass on share-loop (#3) and no pass on at least one of WTP/sponsor (#2). → **Pause product**; document learning; do not “just one more feature.”

---

## Soft pivot options (if kill hits)
| If… | Pivot toward |
|---|---|
| Local WTP dead, tourists click buy | Trip Snapshot only; local graph still free |
| Share works, pay doesn’t | Sponsor-only / restaurant CRM-lite |
| Creators won’t come | Friend-graph only (Tiago); drop marketplace |
| Decide pain weak | Import utility (V1) only — accept smaller ambition |

---

## Flags

**Red:** Moving goalposts (“let’s try Porto first”) after fails is not a pivot — it’s avoidance.  
**Yellow:** Hitting one kill isn’t shame; ignoring it is.
