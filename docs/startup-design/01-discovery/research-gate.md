## What the research found

### Market
Portugal restaurant-discovery as a *paid consumer* category is a **small** pool: TAM ~€28M/yr (Low confidence), SAM ~**€2.5M/yr** / ~**350k** planning users ages 24–34 in AML+AMP (Medium–Low). Year-1 SOM ~**€1,000 net** under organic assumptions. Paid CAC (~€238) vs LTV (€5–15) closes paid acquisition. Ceiling reads as a **profitable niche / lifestyle business**, not a venture-scale Portuguese consumer outcome — unless the model shifts hard toward restaurant-side / sponsored inventory (5–20× larger pool than consumer subs).

### Competition
**Threat: HIGH.** Google Maps + Instagram Saved remain the default free stack. **TheFork Feed** is live in Portugal (Jul 2025). **Uber Eats Top Eats** shipped chef/creator collections in Portugal on **9 Jul 2026**. Creator-sold lists are **not novel** — Beli Paid Guides / Elite exist in the US; World of Mouth already runs a paid curator model with Lisbon talent. B2’s “nobody pays creators” is **false**. Narrow surviving gap: **PT-local creators monetising resident curators** (not restaurants) + **auth-free share** + **Snapshot one-time** as a *bundle* — still weakly evidenced as something locals will buy.

### Demand
Pain language is abundant (save graveyard, “onde vamos comer?”, Maps clutter) — mostly Tier-3 proxies in `raw/customer-voice.md`. Hottest Portuguese signal is **#pub / influencer distrust** (Gasparinho, Abril — Tier 2 press), which supports trust-positioning but does **not** prove WTP. **Zero documented cases of residents paying for a list in their own city.** Travel/tourist WTP is real (Rexby, Spotli.st bands; German search volume into Portugal). Mapstr Plus at **€59/yr** proves some EU users pay for personal maps; reviews scream for **one-time / lifetime** — Snapshot thesis is the clearest monetisation *signal*, not recurring creator subs at €5/mo (discretionary math kills that for modal Inês).

### Timing
Category is crowded with free incumbent discovery layers landing *in-market*. Timing for a “new social discovery app” is poor. Timing for a **distribution wedge** (auth-free `/lists`, WhatsApp, pre-built creator pages, Roleta) is still open *if* the product is useful single-player on day one — which today’s multiplayer-empty promise is not.

## Recommendation: **Yellow light, red-leaning**

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Incumbents already shipping creator curation in PT (TheFork, Uber Eats) | Fatal-adjacent for undifferentiated “social discovery” |
| No local-resident WTP evidence | Load-bearing hole for Snapshot/sub revenue |
| SOM / CAC:LTV arithmetic | Caps ambition; organic-only or die |
| Auth-free share + Snapshot + PT creator share as *bundle* | Real but narrow whitespace — hypothesis, not proof |
| Product already built (v5.7.0) | Sunk cost cuts both ways: cheaper to test, expensive to keep polishing without buyers |

**Proceed to Strategy only if** the founder accepts that Phase 4–8 will design around:
1. **Distribution-first / organic-only** for 90 days (no paid CAC fantasy).
2. **Revenue stack: sponsored / restaurant-side ≥ Snapshot ≫ subscriptions.**
3. **Explicit locals-vs-tourists choice** (brand says local; money evidence says tourist).
4. **Single-player utility before multiplayer network** (Phase 6 must fix empty day-one).
5. **Interviews still the #1 unknown** — desk research cannot close resident WTP.

This is **not** a green light for “raise and scale a Portuguese Beli.” It can still justify a **tight, honest strategy** for a beachhead Lisbon product that either finds paid demand fast or kills the consumer SKU.

**Red light would apply** if the founder needs venture-scale Portugal consumer outcomes, refuses interviews *and* refuses a restaurant/sponsored pivot, or insists creator monthly subs at €5 are the core bet without new evidence.
