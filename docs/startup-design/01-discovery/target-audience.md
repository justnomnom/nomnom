## 4. Anti-persona

1. **Tripadvisor / hotel-concierge tourist** — one-week trip, tourist-menu optimisation. Time Out Market alone ~**4m visitors/year**, 60+ nationalities/week **[Data, Time Out]**. Must not pollute ranking signal.
2. **“Top 50 stars only” Maps user** with no Reels habit — maps closer to **45–64** (social influence on hospitality drops to **57.5%**) **[Data, Marktest]**.

**Nuance (do not over-filter):** Grande Lisboa is **22.6% foreign nationals**; ~**15k** international students/year in Lisbon. **International residents and long-stay students are not anti-personas** — they have high “where do I eat?” pain. Filter on **visit recency / repeat behaviour**, not passport or language. **Confidence: High** on tourist exclusion; Medium on product filter design.

## 6. Jobs-to-be-done

**Functional**
- Find a place I can actually go (map, hours, price band, dietary).
- Turn a Reel/Story into a **stable, shareable place** (not a screenshot).
- Shortlist 2–3 options for a group and get to a booking.
- For weekday: “where near work/campus for lunch?”
- For creators: “answer ‘onde é?’ once with a durable link.”

**Social**
- Look like someone with good taste / local knowledge (not tourist traps).
- Be the organiser who sends one clean list, not ten links.
- Signal trust via *who* recommended, not star average.

**Emotional**
- Confidence before spending scarce discretionary euros.
- Avoid regret / “we got tourist-trapped.”
- Relief when the group decides quickly.

## 8. Buying behaviour + willingness to pay

### How they adopt
- Apps spread via **social feed + peer share**, not App Store search **[Estimate — EU download patterns + PT messaging dominance; PT-specific app-discovery survey = DATA GAP]**.
- Decision criteria (evidence-ordered): (1) survives rich link preview in a group chat, (2) recommendation feels from a real person, (3) free, (4) resolves logistics not just inspiration, (5) shows price.

### Common objections
- “Já tenho isto nos Guardados do Instagram.”
- “Isto é tudo conteúdo pago.” (rational given 26% disclosure)
- “Não há nada perto de mim.” (cold-start / density)
- “Porque é que havia de pagar por isto?”

### WTP — critical finding
**No documented evidence of consumers paying for restaurant lists in the city they already live in.** Every priced precedent is **travel / tourist** context (Rexby lifetime maps, Spotli.st/Tourli guide grids, WoM travel framing). Beli Paid Guides exist (Tier 1 terms) but **publish no prices or adoption data**. **[Data + Data Gap, demand-signals]**

| Revenue line | Evidence strength | Recommended price | Volume confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sponsored restaurant placements** | **Strongest** — food-creator economy already restaurant-funded | Local rates = DATA GAP; anchors micro $150–1,200/post globally | Medium that *model* works; Low on € figure |
| **Snapshot (one-time list)** | Moderate — travel band €8–23; Mapstr users beg for one-time; no competitor offers it | **€3.99–7.99** (ceiling €9.99 flagship); prefer lifetime+updates vs frozen snapshot | **Low** on volume |
| **Creator / consumer subscription** | Weakest for locals | **€2.99–3.99/mo** or **€24.99–29.99/yr** (not €5+) | **Low** on volume |
| Platform consumer sub at €5/mo | Contradicted by discretionary math + Mapstr backlash | **Do not lead** | — |

**Indie reality check:** only verified consumer food-discovery indie revenue found = **$325/30d from 24 subs** (Menu Vista, RevenueCat) **[Data]**. Lifestyle: top 10% of apps take **97.9%** of category revenue **[Data, Adapty]**.

## Strategic Connections

- **Monetisation vs brand:** `BRAND.md` / marketing brief commit to **locals**; WTP evidence points to **tourists**. Phase 4 must choose explicitly (see PROGRESS.md tension) — Snapshot can serve visitors without polluting local ranking if product filters visit intent.
- **Competitors:** TheFork Feed (Jul 2025) and **Uber Eats creator collections in Portugal (9 Jul 2026)** already ship creator-curated discovery with transactions — NomNom cannot win on “we invented curation”; win on **auth-free shareable lists + local trust + creator economics** (see competitor-landscape when synthesised).
- **Pricing vs economics:** Snapshot €3.99–7.99 and sub €2.99–3.99 align with Mapstr/WoM European comps (`demand-signals`) *and* Inês’s €200–400 discretionary reality — but **volume assumptions are the danger**, not the sticker.
- **Occasion → product:** Weekday lunch frequency argues for **near-me / open-now / campus-work** utility, not only weekend “jantar” aspiration — retention model changes if lunch is the hook.
- **Cream / Beli:** Cream warns EGC doesn’t virally scale; Beli grew as **single-player ranking** first. NomNom’s multiplayer cold start is a Phase 6 design constraint (PROGRESS.md).

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- No evidence locals pay for own-city restaurant lists — load-bearing monetisation assumption unevidenced.
- €5/mo primary consumer sub contradicted by income + subscription fatigue.
- Two transaction-owning incumbents shipped creator collections in PT within ~12 months.
- Closest failed analogue (Cream) failed explicitly on WTP + EGC supply shape.

**Yellow Flags:**
- Tourist search volume can inflate demand metrics while poisoning product positioning.
- Primary voice research missing — persona quotes from marketing brief must not be treated as validated.
- Porto SAM rests on unverified AMP population.
- Creator 8k–60k pool may be only tens of good partners — supply concentration risk.
- Giving away unlimited personal saves (category’s proven paywall) is strategically clean vs Mapstr backlash but removes the only proven consumer conversion lever.
