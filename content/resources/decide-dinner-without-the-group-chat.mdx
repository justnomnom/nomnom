Someone has to book by Wednesday. The chat will not. More restaurant links
does not help a group choose; it helps a group stall.

## The unit is a shortlist

Not a dump of Maps pins. Not a 50-best article. **Three to five spots** from a
list whose owner you would trust with the bill. Put group size, neighbourhood,
and last kitchen time in the notes — the stuff “near me” never knows.

## Vote on a Table, not in WhatsApp

1. Build or follow a [list](/features/lists).
2. Open a [Table](/features/table) with those spots.
3. Send the link. Friends can **vote without installing NomNom**.
4. Book what won.

Whoever organises chooses the universe. The group chooses inside it. That split
is the whole product.

## If nobody votes

Two [NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette) spins on the same list, then you leave.
NomNom Roulette on a junk list is still junk.
