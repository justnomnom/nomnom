# Founder observation: Share → Decide (5 silent WhatsApp tests)

Non-code checklist from the Share → Decide spearhead. Run **before** polishing further UX.

## Protocol

1. Build or reuse a **public** list with ≥3 real places the group might go tonight.
2. As organiser, tap **Decide tonight**, then **Share decide link** into a real WhatsApp group (“onde vamos?”).
3. Do **not** coach recipients. Stay silent on how to vote/spin.
4. Observe for one evening (or until someone locks / the group decides offline).

## Pass signals (product)

- Opens per share trending toward ≥1.5 once n≥50 shares
- Recipient activate per open trending toward ≥0.3
- Organiser starts a 2nd list within 14 days (≥15 people as cohort signal)
