# Industry Trends — NomNom

**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Synthesised from:** `raw/trends.md` (A2), `raw/regulatory.md` (A3), `raw/demand-signals.md` (C2)  
**Labels:** **[Data]** sourced claim · **[Estimate]** derived/apportioned · **[Opinion]** interpretive judgment

> This file is intentionally not cheerleading. Three macro forces dominate: AI annexes NomNom’s funnel position (**hurts**); Lisbon authenticity backlash creates a cultural wedge incumbents cannot occupy (**helps**); “lost Instagram saves” is now a commodity feature (**hurts** as a lead claim).

---

## 1. Macro trends

### 1.1 AI discovery is annexing the top of the funnel — HURTS

**[Data]** 44% of Americans plan to use AI more to discover restaurants and book in 2026 (OpenTable / WALR, n=1,527, Sept 2025 fieldwork). **[Data]** 31% of Canadians / **53% of Canadian Gen Z** plan the same while travelling (OpenTable / Ripple, May–June 2026). **[Data]** 83% of restaurant locations are invisible in AI recommendations; top-3 brands capture 53.4% Share of Voice; AI typically returns 3–5 options; rating floors (ChatGPT 4.3+, Perplexity 4.1+, Gemini 3.9+) systematically exclude thin-review independents (Uberall GEO Playbook, May 2026). **[Data]** Google agentic booking (UK Apr 2026) completes reservations via OpenTable/Resy/Tock inside the answer; Yelp licensed 330M reviews + 8M+ listings to OpenAI; OpenTable Concierge lives inside ChatGPT (~60–65k restaurants). **[Data]** 40% trust AI for business recommendations; 42% trust AI as much as traditional reviews; 23% willing to rely solely on AI review summaries (BrightLocal 2026).

**[Opinion]** This is the single most consequential industry force for NomNom. The product’s stated position — inspiration/discovery, transaction elsewhere — is the layer AI assistants are absorbing, and it is the layer DoorDash’s Zesty proved does not monetise. Zero-click / AIO trigger rates for local queries span 11%–78% across low-quality aggregators **[Data — unresolved]**; direction of travel is reliable, any single percentage is not.

**Impact on NomNom:** Primary structural threat. Narrow opportunity only if positioned as the human antidote to AI homogenisation (“the places AI will never show”), not as a better search box.

---

### 1.2 Lisbon authenticity backlash — HELPS

**[Data]** 62.6% of mainland Portugal residents 15–74 ate out on weekdays in the last month (2024) — ten-year high; 25–34 and Greater Lisbon both index above national average (Marktest TGI). **[Data]** June 2025 anti-tourism protests; Airbnb ban in historic downtown; tuk-tuk bans (academic + press). **[Data]** Lisbon &lt;2 traditional cafés per 1,000 residents; Vizinhos em Lisboa maps ~900 surviving traditional cafés (idealista/EFE, Mar 2026). **[Data]** Non-resident arrivals 29.9M in 2025 (+3.3%, decelerating from +9.3%) (INE). **[Data]** Greater Lisbon concentrates tourism stays; Porto domestic overnight stays +19.5% in May 2026 (INE).

**[Opinion]** Strongest single Portugal-specific tailwind. Organised local identity + authenticity demand is something Amex, Google, and OpenAI cannot credibly own. Vizinhos em Lisboa is latent supply, demand, and narrative — not a marketing invention.

**Impact on NomNom:** Favours a Lisbon-local, anti-homogenisation / anti-tourist-trap claim. Does **not** by itself validate consumer willingness to pay for lists.

---

### 1.3 “Lost saves” problem is commoditised — HURTS (as differentiation)

**[Data]** Instagram Saved still has no search/sort/filter as of July 2026 (consistently corroborated; absence of a feature). **[Data]** At least nine utilities attack the same mechanic in 2025–26: Gobbler, dEssence, Reelpin, Tote, Hold My Pin, Sprink, Triply, Stasht, Mapstr — share-to-app → LLM extracts venue → map pin; some free vs Mapstr’s paid tier.

**[Opinion]** The pain is real; the moat is gone. LLM extraction is an API call. Lead with this and NomNom is a feature, not a position. Instagram shipping search-in-saves would collapse much of the category overnight.

**Impact on NomNom:** Keep save-import as table stakes; do not make it the pitch. Defensibility, if any, lives in creator provenance + local curation, not extraction.

## 4. Behavioral shifts

### Trust crisis — real, but not the vacuum the pitch assumes

**[Data]** Google blocked/removed **292M** policy-violating Maps reviews in 2025 (+21% YoY); removed 13M fake Business Profiles (Google Trust & Safety). Tripadvisor removed **2.7M** fraudulent reviews in 2024 (~8% of submissions), 214k AI-generated (Tripadvisor Transparency Report). Honest fake-review range roughly **8%–23%**; figures above ~30% treat as marketing.

**[Data]** Yet **49%** still trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations — BrightLocal series, 15+ years, not cratered. **[Data]** Simultaneously, 40–42% already trust AI recommendations (BrightLocal).

**[Opinion]** Fake reviews are real; consumers absorbed them without abandoning reviews, and are extending fresh trust to AI. Do **not** build the pitch primarily on “trust crisis → pay for human lists.” Reposition around **AI homogenisation / invisible independents**.

### Influencer trust — mixed; paid disclosure hurts conversion

**[Data]** 53% trust a recommendation less if they know the influencer was paid (Clutch, n=277 — small sample). Mintel: influencers drive behaviour but saturation lowers trust. **[Opinion]** Direct tension with marked-sponsored-placement revenue — disclosure is legally mandatory *and* commercially dampening; transparency still beats covert sponsorship (directional evidence only).

### Social-as-search — settled multi-platform, not replacement

Covered in §2. Usage of social for discovery is high among under-35s; preference *over* Google is falling. Inspiration ≠ decision.

### Portuguese dining — demand strong; restaurant B2B stressed

**[Data]** Eating-out at a ten-year high; beachhead demo/geo confirmed (Marktest). **[Data]** 2026: **6,446** openings vs **9,279** closures — net −2,833 (DIG-IN / AHRESP). Food-cost inflation peaks (e.g. dried/salted fish +23.7% H1 2026). Almost three-quarters of surveyed operators expected summer 2026 revenue ≤ summer 2025.

**[Opinion]** Consumer demand for dining exists; restaurants that would buy sponsored placements are margin-compressed and closing faster than opening. B2B monetisation into PT independents in 2026–27 will be hard even though it is the best-*evidenced* revenue pattern in the food-creator economy globally.

### Creator-list WTP — travel yes; locals unevidenced

**[Data]** Category breakouts (Beli, Seekeasy, Crispy List, Menu please!) are free at discovery. **[Data]** Cream (expert-curation analogue) shut down: users wouldn’t pay; EGC doesn’t virality-scale; discovery “not acute enough.” **[Data]** Only verified indie consumer food-app revenue found: Menu Vista **$325/30 days**, 24 subs (RevenueCat). **[Data]** Beli Paid Guides exist (Tier 1 terms) — **no published prices or adoption**. **[Data]** Rexby / Spotli.st / Tourli price bands are **travel** guides (€8–23 typical guidance; live €59 lifetime). **[Data]** Zero documented case of a resident paying for a restaurant list in their own city (C2).

**[Opinion]** Product thesis (named humans &gt; anonymous stars) is supported; monetisation thesis for **local recurring** subscriptions is the weak link. Snapshot / one-time has clearer unserved demand signal (Mapstr users begging for lifetime) than monthly creator subs.

## 5. Regulatory trajectory

Direction of travel: **more platform accountability, earlier VAT/deemed-supplier reality, active Portuguese ad monitoring — but DSA scale obligations stay deferred for micro/small.**

### DSA + `Lei n.º 12-A/2026`

**[Data]** Portugal’s DSA implementing law **Lei 12-A/2026** (15 Apr 2026, in force **20 Apr 2026**): ANACOM as Digital Services Coordinator; fines up to **6%** worldwide turnover (substantive) / **1%** (information duties); compliance-order mechanism with short deadlines. At micro/small: arts. 11–14, 16–18, 24(3) apply; arts. 15, 20–28, 30–32 exempt until scale (+12-month transition). Dark patterns: DSA art. 25 exempt at launch, but **UCPD / DL 57/2008** still binds (Trustpilot / AGCM €4M, Mar 2026 — review authenticity + dark patterns under national consumer law).

### VAT — Fenix / OnlyFans deemed supplier

**[Data]** Impl. Reg. 282/2011 art. 9a: platform presumed deemed supplier of electronically supplied services; irrebuttable if it authorises charge, authorises delivery, or sets T&Cs — upheld in **Fenix International (C-695/20)**. NomNom will do all three → charge VAT on **full** consumer price at buyer’s rate; Union OSS; creator invoices NomNom. From first paid sale.

### Portuguese ad disclosure

**[Data]** `Código da Publicidade` art. 8 — advertising must be unequivocally identified; DGC guide (Oct/Nov 2025): label at **start** (`#Pub` / Publicidade / Patrocinado); platform-native labels alone insufficient. Art. 36: medium-holder (`titular do suporte publicitário`) is co-perpetrator; fines legal person **€3,500–€45,000**. DGC Oct 2025 sweep: influencer post compliance ~90% — enforcement real, currently influencer-directed; platform case law for UGC sponsorship **gap**.

### Other launch-critical constraints (trajectory, not full checklist)

- GDPR/ePrivacy/DPIA for location + social graph; PostHog behind consent.
- App-store IAP: Snapshot/Subscription inside iOS look like digital content → 15–30% of gross (EU external-purchase still ~10–20% + processing, terms volatile).
- Google Maps import: store `place_id` only; do not persist Google Content onto Mapbox.
- DAC7: pre-made lists likely out of scope; custom commissions likely in — confirm with adviser.
- EAA: microenterprise service exemption likely at launch; evaporates at ≥10 staff / €2M.

**[Opinion]** Regulatory risk **Medium**: compliance build is tractable; **commercial** regulatory stack (VAT on gross + store take) can invalidate unit economics. Sequence: UGC + DSA/GDPR basics → validate paid content **on web** → then iOS.

## 6. What this means for NomNom — strategic implications

1. **Reposition the wedge.** Lead with AI homogenisation + Lisbon authenticity (“places AI and tourist algorithms will never surface”), not fake-review rescue and not Instagram-save recovery. **[Opinion]**

2. **Treat save-extraction as table stakes.** Partner or ship parity; never as the hero claim. **[Opinion]**

3. **Move monetisation toward the transaction.** Amex paid $700M for TheFork; Zesty died on unmonetisable discovery. Booking, deposits, waitlists, ticketed events, or Beli-style paid supper club + OpenTable/TheFork partnership beat sponsored placements alone. **[Opinion]** informed by **[Data]**

4. **Do not lean the model on PT sponsored placements.** Entire PT influencer market **€27M** (2025, Primetag); food slice **[Estimate] €2–4M/year**; restaurants closing net-negative; 53% trust paid influencer recs less. Use as a line, not the plan. **[Data]** + **[Estimate]** + **[Opinion]**

5. **Lead consumer monetisation with Snapshot-style one-time (lifetime-with-updates), not local monthly subs.** Unserved demand for one-time is documented; resident recurring WTP is not. Price bands from C2 are hypotheses, not validated PT figures. **[Opinion]**

6. **Build for Beli/World-of-Mouth economics.** Four-person lean ops, profitable small business — unless a transaction wedge changes the ceiling. **[Opinion]**

7. **Ship compliance that unlocks safe harbour early** (DSA notice-and-action, ad labels, DPIA, VAT design before first euro). Cheap vs silent VAT accrual. **[Data]**

8. **Partner Vizinhos em Lisboa / authenticity movements** for supply and distribution before Amex/TheFork or Uber Eats (creator collections already in PT — see competitor synthesis) own the narrative. **[Opinion]**

9. **Resolve locals vs tourists explicitly in Phase 4.** Brand wants locals; WTP and German-language “near me” search volume (~368k/mo in PT) point at visitors. Straddling both without a pricing architecture is a strategy failure mode. **[Data]** + **[Opinion]**

## 9. Strategic Connections and Flags

### Strategic Connections

- **→ competitor-landscape.md:** Uber Eats chef/creator collections in Portugal (9 July 2026) and TheFork Feed already attach curation to a **transaction**. Trends here say capital and users follow that attachment — NomNom discovery-only is structurally late unless it partners or builds a transaction surface. *(PROGRESS; competitor raw)*

- **→ market-analysis / financial:** Lifestyle apps: top 10% capture **97.9%** of category revenue (Adapty). Model volume conservatively; price points can be defensible while volume assumptions kill the case.
