# NomNom — Startup Design Pack

**Generated:** 26 July 2026 · **Mode:** Full · **Language:** English  
**Product reality:** Already shipped (v5.7.0); this pack re-scopes strategy, not greenfield MVP.

---

## Overview

NomNom helps Lisbon friend-groups decide where to eat from spots real people and creators saved — via **auth-free public list links that work before anyone installs anything**. Desk research supports this as a narrow distribution wedge in a **small Portuguese market**, already contested by TheFork Feed and Uber Eats Top Eats. Creator-sold lists are not novel; local resident willingness to pay is unproven. Year-1 economics look like **validation (~€1k net)**, not a salary or a venture outcome.

---

## Key research findings

- TAM ~€28M/yr (Low); SAM ~€2.5M / ~350k planning users; Y1 SOM ~€1k net **[Estimate]**
- Paid CAC ~€238 vs LTV €5–15 → paid growth closed **[Estimate]**
- Mapstr Plus = **€59/yr**; Snapshot/one-time demand visible in reviews **[Data]**
- Beli Paid Guides exist; “nobody pays creators” is false; **no documented own-city resident list purchase**
- Beachhead: Arroios / Campo de Ourique / Alvalade — not tourist core for acquisition

---

## Strategic positioning

**Category:** Auth-free restaurant shortlists for locals (sub-category of group decide, not “social food app”).  
**Revenue stack:** sponsored / restaurant-side ≥ Snapshot ≫ subscriptions.  
**Graph vs money:** locals own the graph; tourists may fund Snapshot as a side channel.

---

## Top 3 risks & mitigations

1. **Local WTP ≈ 0** → Interviews + fake door first; kill consumer SKU if both fail (`06-validation/kill-criteria.md`).
2. **Incumbent free curation** → Don’t compete on “creator lists exist”; win on share/decide + walk-ins.
3. **Share loop fails** → Instrument OG/opens before marketplace polish; kill if opens/share &lt;1.0 @ n=50.

## Anti-patterns detected

- **Boiling the ocean** — six products in one app; spearhead must cut scope (called out in brainstorm).
- **Premature monetisation machinery** — payments designed while webhook 410 and WTP unknown.
- **Solution looking for distribution** — features ahead of share-loop metrics.
- **Vanity metrics risk** — installs/catalogue size vs shares and claims.
- **Ignoring unit economics** — any paid UA plan would violate research (flagged closed).
