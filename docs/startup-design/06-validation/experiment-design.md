# Experiment Design — Top 3

**Phase:** 8 — Validation  
**Date:** 26 July 2026

---

## Experiment A — Customer interviews (n=8)

**Hypothesis:** Lisbon 24–34 diners experience save-graveyard and group-decide pain intensely enough to try an auth-free shortlist; ≥3/8 show non-zero WTP for a €5 Snapshot *or* clear refuse reasons that redirect monetisation.

**Method:** 25–30 min video/in-person; record with consent; notes in `00-intake/interviews/`.

### Script outline
1. Last time you picked a restaurant with friends — walk me through the chat.  
2. Where do you save places now? Show me if willing.  
3. When did retrieval fail?  
4. Who do you trust for food recs? How do you feel about #pub?  
5. If someone dropped a link with 5 places + vote — would you open without an app?  
6. *(Late)* Would you pay €5 once for a great neighbourhood list? Why/why not?  
7. What would make you say no forever?

**Success:** ≥6/8 problem confirm; ≥2 behaviour signals; monetisation clarity (pay or structured no).  
**Timeline:** Days 1–14.  
**Template path:** skill `references/customer-interview.md`.

## Experiment B — Public list share field test

**Hypothesis:** Real groups produce ≥1.5 opens per share and complete a decision in-product.

**Method:**
1. Instrument list open + vote/spin events with `share_id`.  
2. Create 20 lists for real upcoming meals.  
3. Share only via public list links (into real groups).  
4. No pitch for install.

**Metrics:** opens/share, engage rate, qualitative “did you pick?”  
**Pass/fail:** see playbook.  
**Timeline:** Days 7–21 (overlaps interviews).

**OG checklist:** title ≤40 chars; 3 food images; owner name; “Sem app” cue.

---

## Experiment C — Creator pre-build claim

**Hypothesis:** ≥15% of 30 pre-built Lisbon food creators claim within 14 days of DM.

**DM template (PT):**
> Olá [nome] — montámos-te uma página com sítios que recomendaste recentemente: [url]. É tua se quiseres: podes editar, partilhar no bio, zero app obrigatória para quem abre. Se não for útil, ignora — sem hard feelings.

**Tracking sheet columns:** handle | followers | page URL | DM date | viewed | claimed | shared Story | notes  
**Pass/fail:** playbook.  
**Timeline:** Days 14–45.

---

## Flags

Do not run C at scale before B’s OG quality is fixed — creators sharing a bad card burns the only supply channel.
