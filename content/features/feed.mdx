Generic restaurant apps rank. NomNom does not pretend a public average is a
taste. The Discover feed is **the people you follow** — locals and creators —
showing where they actually eat, with their photos and notes.

## What you see

- Spots from people you chose to follow, not an anonymous “near me” stack.
- Context on the card: who saved it, what they said, whether it is already on a
  list you care about.
- A path into [NomNom Circle](/features/feed) on any restaurant — activity from
  *your* people on that spot.

## What you do not see

We do not hide that Discover can also carry labelled sponsored placements.
Creator lists stay editorial. If a placement is paid, it is marked. See
[why star ratings stopped meaning much](/resources/why-star-ratings-stopped-meaning-anything).
