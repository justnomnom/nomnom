# Value Proposition — NomNom

**Phase:** 4 — Strategy  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Confidence:** Medium (pain language); Low (WTP / gains willingness to pay)

---

## One-sentence value prop

**NomNom turns “onde vamos comer?” into a shared, trusted shortlist — from people you actually believe — that opens from a public link without an install.**

---

## Value Proposition Canvas

### Customer Profile (Inês / Tiago — Lisbon residents)

#### Jobs-to-be-done
| Type | Job |
|---|---|
| Functional | Pick a place tonight / this week that the group will accept; retrieve a saved Reel when in the neighbourhood |
| Social | Not be the person who picks the tourist trap or the dud; look like someone with good taste |
| Emotional | Reduce decision anxiety; feel that picks are “real,” not #pub |

#### Pains
| Pain | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp veto loop / polite deadlock | High (episodic) | Tier-3 voice + persona brief |
| Can’t find the saved place in 400 Saves | Medium–High | Gobbler/Triply proxies; category swarm |
| Distrust of gifted creators | Hot in PT hospitality discourse | Gasparinho / Abril **[Data, Tier 2]** |
| Tab stack: IG → Maps → TheFork → WhatsApp | Medium | Observed behaviour |
| Empty social app on day one | High for NomNom specifically | Product structure vs Beli |

#### Gains
| Gain | Type |
|---|---|
| Group agrees in &lt;10 minutes | Required |
| Link works for friends who never heard of NomNom | Required |
| Source of each pick is a named person | Expected (trust-first brand) |
| Personal archive becomes searchable / visitable | Desired |
| One-time buy of a great list when travelling | Desired (tourist / trip context) |
| Creator gets a permanent home for recommendations | Desired (supply side) |

### Value Map

#### Products & services
Auth-free NomNom Lists · NomNom Roulette · public `/u/handle` · personal saves/import · (later) Snapshot · marked sponsorship

#### Pain relievers → pains
| Reliever | Pain |
|---|---|
| Shared list + vote/Roulette in one link | Group deadlock |
| Places-as-objects (not video graves) | Save retrieval failure |
| Named human provenance, #pub labelled | Trust vacuum |
| Pre-seeded personal / creator content | Empty day-one feed |

#### Gain creators → gains
| Creator | Gain |
|---|---|
| Public-link OG cards (Remotion) | Friends engage without install |
| Unlimited free personal saves | Archive utility without Mapstr 300-cap tax |
| Snapshot SKU | Pay once when value is clear |
| Creator claim flow | Supply gets durable asset |

### Fit assessment
**Problem–solution fit (desk):** Strong on group decide + trust language.  
**Product–market fit:** Untested — especially resident pay.  
**Channel–product fit:** Strong *if* share mechanics + OG are excellent — this is the real product bet. **[Opinion]**

## Proof points & credibility (today vs needed)

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| “Works without install” | Shipped routes exist — must prove share UX |
| “From people you trust” | Positioning only until density + disclosure |
| “Creators get paid” | Designed; payments webhook 410; WTP open |
| “Locals, not traps” | Earn with density; do not badge until criterion exists (`BRAND.md`) |

---

## Flags

**Red:** Paying for local lists unproven — do not centre value prop on “buy lists.”  
**Yellow:** Tourist Snapshot gains conflict with local brand if over-weighted in marketing.
