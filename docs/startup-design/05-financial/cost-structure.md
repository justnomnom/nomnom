## Break-even (honest)
- **Infra-only break-even:** trivial (~€100/mo) — meaningless.  
- **Founder-salary break-even** (e.g. €2k/mo net): requires sponsor-led model, not Snapshot crumbs — **unlikely Year 1** on research SOM.  
- **Strategic break-even:** share loop + WTP signal — non-€ metric.
