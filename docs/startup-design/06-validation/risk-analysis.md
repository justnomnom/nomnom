## P0 mitigations & early warnings

### Local WTP ≈ 0
- **Mitigate:** Interview + fake door early; pivot to sponsor-only or freeze consumer SKU  
- **Warning:** 0/8 would pay; fake-door CTR &lt;1%

### Incumbent free curation
- **Mitigate:** Position on group decide + walk-in venues; never claim “we invented creators”  
- **Warning:** Users say “I already see this on Uber Eats”

### Creator supply loss
- **Mitigate:** Pre-build show-don’t-tell now; pay only after model works  
- **Warning:** Claim rate &lt;5%; Top Eats exclusivity rumours

### Share loop fail
- **Mitigate:** Prioritise OG/analytics before features  
- **Warning:** Opens/share &lt;1.0 after 50 shares

### Runway
- **Mitigate:** Founder fills intake; time-box 90 days  
- **Warning:** No calendar for interviews
