## 1. How money comes in (priority order)

### A. Sponsored / restaurant-side (primary ambition)
- Marked placements on creator or neighbourhood lists; fixed fee, not per-cover (contrast TheFork €2–4/cover resentment).
- Transparency mandatory — platform co-liable under PT publicidade rules. **[Data]**
- Pool 5–20× larger than consumer subs at national scale. **[Estimate]** geography synthesis.

### B. Snapshot — one-time list purchase (primary *consumer* SKU to test)
- €3.99–7.99 typical; €9.99 flagship ceiling. **[Assumption]** aligned to travel guide bands + Mapstr one-time demand.
- Best fit: trip lists, neighbourhood deep-dives, creator “greatest hits” — **tourist or trip context first**; local resident purchase is the open experiment.
- Mapstr has **no** lifetime SKU at €59/yr Plus — gap is real. **[Data]**

### C. Subscriptions (deprioritised)
- Platform or creator subs ≤ €2.99–3.99/mo if tested. **[Assumption]**
- Modal Lisbon discretionary €200–400 makes €5/mo indefensible. **[Data]** audience.
- Beli Elite ~$3.99/$29.99 is the global price memory — do not exceed without proof. **[Data]**

### Explicit non-model (Year 1)
Paid user acquisition. CAC ~€238 vs LTV €5–15 → closed. **[Estimate]**

## 3. Scalability
- Portugal alone = lifestyle / niche ceiling unless Iberia expansion after Lisbon density.
- Network effects are **local** (neighbourhood clusters ~1k–3k MAU per self-sustaining node). **[Estimate]**
- Auth-free pages scale distribution without app install friction; graph density does not scale with paid ads.
