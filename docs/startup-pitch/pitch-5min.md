*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 26 July 2026*

## 0:00–0:40 — What you do

NomNom helps people in Lisbon decide where to eat from spots creators and friends they trust actually saved — not anonymous Google stars. Lists, profiles, and Roulette open from a public link — no app install required.

**Proof moment:** Group stuck deciding → one list link → shortlist or Roulette → done. No download.

**Note:** Pause. Don’t say “Portuguese Beli.”

---

## 0:40–1:40 — Insight (lead)

Category killers die on **distribution**, not features. DoorDash shut Zesty in ~4 months. Beli, TheFork Feed, and Uber Eats Top Eats (PT, July 2026) already do creator curation.

**Our wedge:** Auth-free trusted lists + walk-in venues booking/delivery ignore + paying local curators — not inventing “lists.”

---

## 1:40–2:20 — Problem

Saving is easy; deciding with friends is not. Stack today: IG Saved + Maps lists + screenshots + group-chat chaos → tourist-trap fatigue pick.

---

## 2:20–3:10 — Solution

Public lists, creator profiles, Roulette — shipped (v5.7.0). Install is optional upgrade. Monetisation designed; **demand unproven** — we say so.

---

## 3:10–3:40 — Why now (one beat)

AI local recs jumped to ~45% of consumers **[BrightLocal 2026]** while ~83% of restaurants stay invisible in AI answers **[Uberall]**. Human, attributable taste matters more as AI shortlists homogenise — especially in Lisbon.

---

## 3:40–4:10 — Market + model (honest)

Portugal platform pool ~€28M; SAM ~350k diners; consumer-sub ceiling ~€60k/yr. **Not** a €50B story. Money: marked sponsorship ≥ Snapshot one-time ≫ subs. **No paid ads** (CAC inverted).
