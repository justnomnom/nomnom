Indecision is not a flavour. **NomNom Roulette** is a random pick from a pool
that still belongs to your graph: people you follow, lists you already trust.

Spin for a pick. No overthinking — just NomNomming.

## What it is

- A spinner, not a search ranking.
- The pool is not “whatever scored 4.7 citywide”.
- Two spins is plenty. If the table is still split, put the finalists on a
  [Table](/features/table) and vote.

Lisbon has a public page at [/roleta/lisboa](/roleta/lisboa) for a taste of the
mechanic. Signed-in NomNom Roulette lives in the app and follows *your* people.

## What it is not

NomNom Roulette will not rescue a bad list. Put honest spots in, then spin. A ritual
helps: Friday 19:00, one list, two spins, leave the house.
