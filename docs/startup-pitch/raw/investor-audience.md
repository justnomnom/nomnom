### Fundraising Landscape

**Climate in Portugal / Iberia consumer & early-stage (2025–2026):** Neutral-to-selective. Local capital remains available for Iberia-rooted pre-seed/seed tech, but checks concentrate on fewer companies with clearer distribution and validation signals. **What VCs look for in consumer social / local discovery at pre-seed (2025–2026 consensus):**
1. **Distribution insight > product demo** — “why customers will find *you*” (organic loops, creators, WhatsApp/community, UGC). [Opinion consensus — VC Cafe Jul-era 2026; Visible.vc consumer VC guide; Round Funded]

### Current Climate Notes

**Europe fundraising 2026:** Capital exists but is **concentrated** — fewer startups funded, larger rounds for winners; early-stage diligence longer (often multi-month; some guides say plan **~9 months** before cash need). AI absorbs a large share of VC value; non-AI consumer faces a higher bar. [Data / Estimate — Dealroom commentary 2026; Grantbite Europe VC 2026; SeedScope trends]

**Consumer social specifically:** Category widely described as **oversaturated / cooled** for generic social platforms; active investor lists shrunk vs prior cycle. Exceptions: creator tools with monetization, niche real-world communities, commerce-enabled social. [Opinion / Data — VC Boom 2026; New Market Pitch social commerce & creator trends; Business Insider creator analysis]

**Local marketplace / restaurant discovery after Zesty:**
- DoorDash launched **Zesty** (AI social restaurant discovery) Dec 2025; wound down **~17 Apr 2026** (<6 months), folding conversational search & personalized recs into core DoorDash. [Data — TechCrunch Dec 2025; NRN / Restaurant Technology News Apr 2026]
- Narrative for investors: **standalone discovery apps struggle for habitual adoption** against super-apps; discovery is consolidating into platforms that own book/order/pay. [Opinion — RTN analysis; SOUS/Devon Scoulelis LinkedIn]
- Counter-narrative for NomNom: Europe/SME restaurants often prefer **open stacks** over US-style super-app lock-in; trust/creator/WOM discovery remains a gap Google ads don’t fill; auth-free public-link distribution is a regional wedge DoorDash/Zesty never owned. [Opinion — pitch framing]

**Hot topics for PT/Iberia investors 2026:** AI applied to real workflows, capital efficiency, Iberia → Europe expansion stories, ESG where relevant, founder velocity. [Opinion]

**Fatigue topics:** Generic “AI-powered” decks (Round Funded: “AI-powered” in ~71% of decks), paid-growth consumer social, unfocused TAM slides, idea-stage with no distribution plan. [Data / Opinion]

**Fundraising difficulty vs 12 months ago:** For non-AI consumer discovery — **harder / more selective**, not frozen. Southern Europe still producing discovery raises (Bestie Bite 2026). [Estimate]

### Implications for NomNom Pitch

Concrete framing for **Portuguese angels + Bynd-band + Portugal Ventures-type + Iberia accelerators** (not US Series A consumer VCs):

1. **Lead with distribution, not “social network.”** Open with auth-free public lists, profiles, Roulette — proof users can share *before* install. Frame as **distribution infrastructure for local taste**, not another feed. [Opinion]

2. **Position the round correctly.** €200k = classic PT **angel / micro pre-seed** to prove city density + creator loop. If targeting Bynd as lead, consider **€250–400k** to clear their ticket floor, or present angels filling to €250k+ with Bynd option. Use Bloop (€1.4M) as aspirational *next* round, Lunia (€300k) as peer. [Estimate]

3. **Use comps surgically.** Beli = category proof (social lists work); Mapstr = Europe place-sharing; Bestie Bite = Southern EU still funds hospitality discovery; Zesty shutdown = **moat is trust + distribution channel, not AI search UX**. Avoid claiming you are “the Beli of Portugal” without Portugal density metrics. [Opinion]

4. **Show behavior metrics they can underwrite without revenue.** Weekly active creators, organic list shares, Roulette sessions, return rate, city heatmap (Lisbon/Porto first). Pre-empt “no traction” with instrumented product (v5.7.0 is an asset — demo live). [Opinion]

5. **Solo founder slide:** Acknowledge risk; show shipping cadence, named advisors (food / growth / PT ecosystem), 90-day hire (community/GTM), and why technical depth is the constraint at pre-seed. [Opinion]

6. **Portugal Ventures path:** Emphasize Portuguese HQ, MVP live, market feedback plan, complementary skills (advisors), and co-investor interest; accept board/tranche reality. Tourism + Digital framing if discovery-for-travelers is honest. [Assumption]

7. **Accelerator path:** From Start-to-Table for foodtech network and investor density — not as substitute for the raise. [Data]

8. **Avoid US Series A language:** No “need $2M seed for national blitz.” Speak **city density → Iberia → Europe**, capital-efficient, creator/organic-share CAC. [Opinion]

9. **Monetization honesty:** Pre-revenue OK at true pre-seed; show 2–3 model options (creator subs, venue promo, affiliate/booking) with a 12–18 month experiment plan — not a fake MRR chart. [Opinion]

10. **Climate jab that helps:** “DoorDash killed standalone Zesty because discovery without habitual distribution dies. NomNom starts with trusted creators/friends and auth-free public lists — no install — where Iberians already coordinate plans.” [Opinion]

- Pitching primarily to US consumer Series A firms at this stage (wrong check size, wrong traction bar, wrong geo). [Opinion]
- Zero usage instrumentation at “product built” stage — investors will assume no learning loop. [Opinion]
- Solo founder with no hiring/advisor mitigation on the team slide (~directional 21% pass reason). [Data / Tier 3]
- Framing as generic consumer social without creator monetization or local marketplace path (category fatigue 2026). [Opinion]
- Ignoring Bynd’s €250k floor if Bynd is the named lead target. [Data vs ask mismatch]
- Portugal Ventures application without market feedback story (explicit FAQ rejection theme). [Data]
- Over-claiming World of Mouth or Mapstr funding figures (sources conflict / outdated). [Data integrity]

### Yellow Flags
- Pre-launch / pre-revenue is acceptable for PT €200k angels but **tight** for Indico-style seed preferences. [Data / Estimate]
- COREangels Food thesis skews AgTech/supply — discovery may need a “Future of Food discovery” narrative bridge. [Assumption]
- Consumer seed medians globally are soft; plan longer process (4–9 months) and bridge grants (StartUp Voucher €5k is symbolic only). [Data / Estimate]
- Zesty shutdown can be spun against *all* discovery apps — must own the counter-story in the deck. [Opinion]
- Grantbite claim that many domestic funds want **€10–50k MRR before seed** — verify per fund; do not treat as universal pre-seed bar. [Data — single Tier 2/3 guide; cross-check in meetings]
