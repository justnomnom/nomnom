## 1. Competitive overview

The landscape is **crowded at the free-habit layer**, **fragmented and under-capitalised in social discovery apps**, and **concentrated among Portugal incumbents that own the transaction**.

| Layer | Who | Concentration |
|---|---|---|
| Status quo (default behaviour) | Instagram/TikTok saves, Google Maps lists, WhatsApp, screenshots | Near-total among 24–34 urban diners |
| PT transaction incumbents | TheFork (booking + Feed), Uber Eats (Top Eats, 9 Jul 2026) | High — local supply, language, distribution |
| PT editorial / media | Time Out Lisboa, NiT, Lifecooler | High on head SEO terms |
| Social discovery apps | Beli (US), Mapstr (EU), Barhio + Spanish cluster, WoM, importers (~20) | Fragmented; no funded consolidator |
| PT native discovery | DIG-IN (ex-Zomato) | Weak / maintenance mode |

**Overall threat: HIGH for Portugal incumbents** (Google Maps, TheFork Feed, Uber Eats Top Eats), not because they will build NomNom’s full product tomorrow, but because they already occupy distribution, restaurant supply, and — as of mid-2025 / July 2026 — **creator-curated discovery inside apps Portuguese diners already use**. The app-layer field is barbell-shaped: unassailable free defaults on one end; small, lean, mostly unfunded tools on the other. DoorDash’s Zesty (Dec 2025 → shut 17 Apr 2026) is the control experiment that pure demand-side, second-app discovery fails even with unlimited budget.

**Critical reframe (PROGRESS-corrected):** creator-sold restaurant lists are **not novel**. Beli ships Paid Guides; Uber Eats signed Portuguese creators into free curation on 9 July 2026; World of Mouth runs paid/credited expert curation with Lisbon coverage. Agent B2’s claim that “nobody pays creators” is **false**. The surviving whitespace is much narrower: **local creators in a non-English market, monetising curators (not restaurants), for diners who already live in that city** — a case with no documented priced precedent in the research set.

## 4. Competitor GTM summary

### Beli — copy mechanics, not outcome

| Mechanic | Replicable in Lisbon? |
|---|---|
| Single-player utility on day one (rank/save alone) | **Yes — non-negotiable** |
| ~80% referral; coercive **four-invite** onboarding gate | Mechanics yes; **outcome no** (Lisbon pool ~2.8M metro; campus/Hinge density unavailable) |
| Founder-led IG/TikTok dining content; almost zero paid | Yes |
| Watch organic city growth, then show up | Yes |
| Become a personality/status signal | **Not plannable** — output of US Gen Z scale |
| Invite-only waitlist scarcity myth | **Drop** — contradicted by founder interviews |

**Blunt verdict (B3):** Beli’s *tactics* are cheap and transferable. Beli’s *outcome* is not a NomNom base case. Model referral as an efficiency multiplier, not the growth engine.

### Mapstr — transferable campaign; pricing warning

- Organic + **co-branded tourism-board maps** (CRT Bretagne: 1.5M impressions, 30k map clicks) — maps almost 1:1 onto NomNom auth-free `/lists/:id`.
- Freemium pivot: ~1,000 insult emails/day; paid accepted **3× better in UK/US than France** → Portuguese WTP risk for consumer subs is material.
- ~15% MAU/registered (historical France proxy) is a sobering activation benchmark.

### Zero-budget channels that actually fit (ranked)

1. Be creator zero for ~90 days (public list + content).
2. Hand-recruit 10–20 nano/micro creators (3k–30k); founding deal + bio-ready `/u/:handle` — lead with tool value, not unproven rev-share.
3. Auth-free public lists + invite/referral gate (value before install).
4. Honest presence in r/lisboa, FB groups, coworkings.
5. Long-tail programmatic SEO (not head terms — Time Out/NiT own those).
6. Mapstr-style institutional co-created map.
7. Concentrated PR (NiT, Time Out, Observador, ECO, Shifter) with data stories from own DB.
8. Portuguese Instagram DM extraction bot (Hold My Pin pattern; nobody owns Lisbon-PT version).

**Do not position on Reel→map import** — commoditised and free. **Defer** paid social and restaurant field sales until density exists. Zesty: second-app, demand-only launch is dead.

### Media / TheFork GTM notes

- **NiT / Time Out:** SEO + brand; free consumer; ad-funded. Contestable only on long-tail, freshness, creator-attribution queries, and tools (`/roleta/`).
- **TheFork:** supply-led B2B + **Yums** discounts; PT Yum redemption worth **half** FR/DE (€10/€25 vs €20/€50) — hard signal that PT consumer LTV is lower.

## 6. Switching cost analysis

**Why people stay on the status quo**

1. Free, pre-installed, zero decision cost (IG + Google Maps + WhatsApp).
2. **Pain is temporally displaced** from the save action — cost paid weeks later in another context. Hardest structural problem in the category (~20 importer apps, no runaway winner).
3. Capture moment defended by the scroll — share-sheet is table stakes and already free.
4. Sunk lists in Google Maps (import parity required, not a marketing story).
5. “Good enough” for low-discovery diners — addressable segment is high discovery volume + loss anxiety, not everyone who eats.

**What actually switches them (evidence-ranked)**

1. **A person, not a feature** — creator they follow: “my lists live here” (bypasses temporal displacement).
2. Proximity resurfacing (Stasht already does this free).
3. Group decision resolution (acute WhatsApp pain *now*).
4. Been / want-to-go state (Google still unfixed).
5. Named local trust vs polluted aggregate / AI consensus.

**Migration frame loses.** Win by arriving as a creator’s list someone opens because they follow that creator; only later become the save destination. Distribution through creator audiences is the **entry vector**, not a channel add-on.

## 7. Strategic recommendations

### Where to compete

| Bet | Why |
|---|---|
| **Portugal-first density** (Lisbon beachhead, then Porto) | Beli has no PT footprint; window is local supply exclusivity |
| **Creator revenue share that beats badge/podcast exposure** | Uber Eats and TheFork recruit with distribution, not list GMV |
| **Auth-free shareable lists** | Beli-weak; Mapstr Bretagne playbook; PT link-share / group-chat behaviour |
| **Snapshot one-time SKU** | Documented unmet demand in Mapstr reviews; Mapstr explicitly no lifetime |
| **Walk-in / non-delivery / non-bookable venues** | Structural blind spot of Uber + TheFork |
| **Unlimited free personal saves** | Explicit contrast to Mapstr’s 300 cap |

### Where to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Claiming “we invented creator-sold lists” | Beli Paid Guides + Uber PT collections — investor-checkable |
| Differentiating on Reel/TikTok capture | Commoditised free (~20 apps + Hold My Pin DM) |
| Competing for generic “where to eat” vs Ask Maps / chatbots | Homogenised AI consensus; be the **cited source** |
| Head SEO (“melhores restaurantes Lisboa”) | Time Out / NiT closed for a solo founder |
| Discount / booking wars with TheFork | Incumbent co-finances from Tripadvisor P&L |
| Consumer sub priced far above **$3.99/mo / $29.99/yr** creator-guide anchor without justification | Beli sets the global price memory |
| Modelling Beli’s growth curve as base case | Mechanics ≠ outcome in Lisbon |

### Surviving wedge (synthesis)

> **Portugal + local creator revenue share + auth-free share + Snapshot one-time** — the combination still largely unserved. Individually, pieces exist (Beli monetises creators in the US; Uber/TheFork do free PT curation; Mapstr proves EU paid saves; Rex/Dinelist prove lifetime SKU shape). **Nobody combines local PT creator payouts, city-you-live-in lists, auth-free distribution, and one-time purchase.**

Price inside the observed band (bottom for PT disposable income). Recruit PT creators **now** before Uber exclusivity habits lock — named Top Eats creators are currently paid in exposure. Exploit restaurant resentment of TheFork’s €2–4/cover for marked sponsorship **without** per-cover fees — with radical transparency (WoM has made “no sponsored lists” a vocal counter-position).

**Moat (if any):** local creator density/exclusivity and two-sided liquidity in one city — not features, not the concept, not “first in Portugal” (By Chefs and Uber arrived July 2026).
