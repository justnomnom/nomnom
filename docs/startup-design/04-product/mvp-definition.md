# MVP Re-scope — NomNom

**Phase:** 6 — Product *(adapted: product already at v5.7.0 — this is a spearhead re-scope, not greenfield MVP)*  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Hypothesis under test:** An auth-free, shareable shortlist from trusted humans beats Maps-pin spam for Lisbon group decisions — and can acquire users without paid CAC.

---

## Core hypothesis
If we make **sharing + deciding** excellent and **day-one single-player** non-empty, then Lisbon organisers will reuse NomNom public list links within 90 days — creating organic opens — *before* we need creator paid lists to work.

**Falsifiers:** opens/share &lt; 1.0; creators won’t claim pre-built pages; organisers don’t create a second list; zero WTP in interviews *and* zero sponsor interest.

---

## Must-have (spearhead “MVP”)
*Ship or harden — not invent from zero.*

1. **Auth-free list view** that is fast, mobile-perfect, no login wall.
2. **Public list share** with rich Remotion (or equivalent) OG: title, count, 3–4 images, creator/owner name.
3. **Group decide mechanic** — vote/veto and/or NomNom Roulette from the shared list.
4. **Single-player value** — personal saves list useful alone; optional Maps import path so feed isn’t empty.
5. **Public `/u/handle`** claimable by creators; founder can pre-seed places.
6. **Analytics** — share → open → activate (edit/vote/spin/save/claim).
7. **Disclosure pattern** for any sponsored item (even if inventory empty).

---

## Nice-to-have (v1.1 — after share loop)
- Live Snapshot checkout (re-enable Stripe webhooks) on 1–2 flagship lists
- Creator payout dashboard (only after take-rate model survives VAT+store math)
- Social follow graph / Circle as *secondary*
- Deep Reel/TikTok parse reliability
- “Locals eat here” signal (needs density + criterion)

---

## Explicitly out of scope (90 days)
- Paid UA campaigns
- Booking / delivery rails
- Head-term SEO war
- National/Iberia expansion
- AI “best restaurants” chatbot
- Mapstr-style 300-cap paywall on personal saves
- Claiming category invention vs Beli/Uber

---

## Success criteria (90 days)

| Signal | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Opens per share (n≥50) | ≥1.5 | &lt;1.0 |
| Creators claimed | ≥10 | &lt;5 |
| Organisers with 2nd list in 14d | ≥15 | &lt;5 |
| Interview WTP or sponsor LOI | ≥3/8 would pay *or* 1 paid restaurant | Neither |
| Paying transactions | ≥5 Snapshots *or* €500 sponsor | €0 and no pipeline |

Product already “built” is not success. **Shared decisions** are.

---

## Flags

**Red:** Multiplayer-empty day one remains the Beli lesson — must-have #4 is non-negotiable.  
**Yellow:** Resist polishing Capacitor parity while web share is weak.
