# Brand Personality — NomNom

**Phase:** 5 — Brand  
**Date:** 26 July 2026

---

## Archetype
**Primary: The Regular (Everyperson) + splash of Jester**  
Accessible friend who knows good tables — not the critic priest (Sage-only) and not the luxury Concierge. Playfulness = Jester; trust = Everyperson.

**Why not Sage/Expert:** WoM and Time Out own “editor knows best.” NomNom’s bet is *your people*, not another critic.

**Why not Rebel:** Anti-#pub energy is useful, but full Rebel becomes competitor-bashing (banned by `BRAND.md`).

---

## Emotional attributes
- Relief (decision made)
- Warm social belonging (group agreed)
- Light delight (Roulette, “Nom Nom”)
- Quiet confidence (named source)

Avoid: FOMO urgency, exclusivity flex, tourist-guide gloss on local surfaces.

---

## Visual direction (not a full redesign)
- Keep existing product system (`DESIGN.md` / theme tokens) — don’t invent a second brand for the pitch.
- **Share surfaces are the brand:** Remotion OG cards must show list name, 3–4 plates/places, human handle — readable at link-preview thumbnail size.
- Atmosphere: real Lisbon food photography &gt; abstract gradients; residential neighbourhoods &gt; postcard Baixa.
- Motion: intentional (Roulette spin, list appear) — not noise.

vs competitors:
| Brand | Feel |
|---|---|
| TheFork | Utility booking, deals |
| Uber Eats | Logistics, hunger now |
| Beli | Rank-obsessed social score |
| Mapstr | Personal map nerd utility |
| **NomNom** | Friendly group decide + trusted names |
