# Positioning — NomNom (April Dunford)

**Phase:** 4 — Strategy  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Confidence:** Medium — research-backed alternatives; unique attributes partly shipped, partly aspirational

---

## 1. Competitive alternatives
*(What people do if NomNom doesn’t exist)*

1. **Paste three Google Maps links into WhatsApp** and hope.
2. **Scroll Instagram Saves / ask the group for a Reel** and lose the place name.
3. **Open TheFork Feed or Uber Eats Top Eats** — free creator curation attached to book/order.
4. **Ask ChatGPT / Google AI** for “best dinner Lisbon” — homogenised consensus.
5. **Follow NiT / Time Out / a trusted human** and DM them.
6. **Do nothing** — default to the same three safe places.

Direct apps (Beli, Mapstr, WoM, Barhio) are alternatives for *power users*, not for the modal group-chat decision.

---

## 2. Unique attributes
*(Factual / near-factual — not slogans)*

| Attribute | Status |
|---|---|
| Public auth-free list & profile URLs that render full content | **Shipped** |
| Group-oriented Roulette / decide-from-link path | **Partially shipped** |
| Portugal-first UX, language, neighbourhood framing | **Shipped intent** |
| Creator revenue share on sold lists (vs exposure-only Uber) | **Designed, not live** |
| Unlimited free personal saves (vs Mapstr 300 cap) | **Product stance** |
| Snapshot one-time SKU | **Designed, payments off** |

**Not unique (do not claim):** creator-sold lists; Reel capture; “social restaurant discovery.”

---

## 3. Value
*(Attributes → outcomes)*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Auth-free lists | Friends decide without downloading; every share is acquisition |
| Named human provenance | Lower fear of #pub / tourist-trap pick |
| Creator pages + possible pay | Creators stop answering the same DM; may earn (if WTP) |
| Free unlimited saves | Personal utility day one — not paywalled archive |
| Snapshot | Pay once when the list is worth it — no €59/yr Mapstr tax |

---

## 4. Target customer
**Best-fit:** Lisbon 24–34 resident who organises or participates in group dinners weekly/biweekly, already saves food content, lives outside pure tourist core, and will open a shared list link tonight.

**Best-fit supply:** Lisbon food creators 3k–50k with local engagement (not travel-only accounts).

**Not the launch target:** National SEO tourists searching “melhores restaurantes Lisboa”; users who only want delivery; booking-deal hunters.

---

## 5. Market category

**Choice: New sub-category of an existing habit**

> **Auth-free restaurant shortlists for locals** — not “another social food app,” not “Portuguese Beli.”

Framing that makes value obvious: *the thing you send when the group can’t decide* — adjacent to group polls and Maps pins, not adjacent to TikTok.

**Avoid:** inventing a new category (“creator commerce OS for food”) — expensive education, Zesty-shaped death.

---

## Positioning statement (working)

**For** Lisbon friend-groups stuck in “onde vamos comer?”,  
**NomNom** is the **auth-free shareable shortlist**  
**that** turns trusted people’s saved spots into a decidable public link  
**unlike** Maps pin spam, TheFork/Uber free feeds, or app-walled social food apps  
**because** anyone can open, vote, and spin — no install, named sources, Portugal-first.
