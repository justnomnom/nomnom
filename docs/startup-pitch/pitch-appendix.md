## Top 10 investor questions

### 1. How is this different from TheFork Feed / Uber Eats Top Eats?
**Answer:** They validate creator curation *inside* booking and delivery. We own **auth-free trusted lists** (decide before install) and **walk-in / non-delivery** Lisbon venues those funnels under-serve. We also aim to pay curators via Snapshot/sponsorship GMV, not only exposure badges. **[Opinion + Data on launches]**

### 2. Isn’t this just Beli for Portugal?
**Answer:** Beli proves friends > stars and Paid Guides. We are not claiming to invent that. Our raise is for **PT auth-free distribution + one-city density**, not a US social graph copy. If Beli localises tomorrow, density and creator relationships are the moat — features aren’t. **[Opinion]**

### 3. What traction do you have?
**Answer:** Product-built, **pre-revenue / no disclosed MAU** in this materials set. Signal today = shipping velocity (~4 months to v5.7.0). Next milestones are creators live, share CTR, cluster MAU, first paid euro. **[Assumption on traction status]**

### 4. Will locals pay for restaurant lists?
**Answer:** **Unknown — that’s the experiment.** No documented WTP for lists in the city you already live in; travel precedents exist. We lead with **free consumer + marked sponsorship + Snapshot** (trip context first), not €5/mo subs. **[Data gap owned]**

### 5. Why won’t Google / Instagram kill you?
**Answer:** They are the default save layer and always will be. We don’t win by better filing cabinets; we win when a **person you follow** says “my list lives here” and the group opens a link. Import parity is table stakes, not the story. **[Opinion]**

### 6. Market feels tiny — why invest?
**Answer:** Agree Portugal consumer discovery alone is niche (~€28M platform TAM estimate; ~€60k/yr consumer-sub ceiling). This cheque funds a **Lisbon proof**. Upside paths: Iberia density playbook or restaurant-side revenue (5–20× pool). Wrong fund if you need €100M outcome from PT diners alone. **[Estimate]**

### 7. Solo founder risk?
**Answer:** Real. Category dies on distribution. Round funds creator GTM capacity (hire/co-founder/advisors), not more engineering surface. **[Assumption]**

### 8. What about Barhio in Barcelona?
**Answer:** Same zero-install share narrative — we do **not** claim inventing that mechanic. Differentiation = Lisbon creators + friends + Snapshot commerce + residential graph discipline. Watch Barhio for Iberia expansion. **[Data + Opinion]**

### 9. Unit economics / CAC?
**Answer:** Paid CAC estimated ~€238 vs LTV €5–15 — **closed**. Plan is organic-only 90+ days. **[Estimate]**

### 10. Why now vs too late?
**Answer:** Partially timed: AI shortlists concentrate (BrightLocal 6%→45%; Uberall ~83% restaurants invisible in AI). Incumbents shipping creator layers = validation *and* threat. We lead with distribution insight, not a “wide open market” claim. **[Data]**

4. **Founder bio placeholders** — fill before send.
