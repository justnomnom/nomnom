## 1. Overview

Most of what NomNom can treat as *known* is the competitive and regulatory surface: two transaction-owning incumbents already ship creator-curated discovery in Portugal; free status-quo tools own habit; VAT deemed-supplier and DSA/ad-label obligations are readable from primary law; eating-out demand among 25–34s in Greater Lisbon is Tier-1 demography. That ground is solid enough to reject novelty claims, reject paid acquisition as Year-1 growth, and reject a Portugal-only venture-scale consumer-sub story.

Almost everything that decides whether NomNom is a business — resident willingness to pay for own-city lists, Portugal CAC/retention, creator imported-social conversion, beachhead freguesia fit, and TheFork Feed *usage* (not mere presence) — sits on estimates, adjacent European/US proxies, or unanswered searches. `raw/customer-voice.md` is now on disk but is **Tier-3 proxy voice**, not Portuguese diner interviews — treat primary WTP validation as still open. Year-1 SOM ~€1k and the ~20× CAC/LTV inversion are arithmetically coherent but rest on global freemium benchmarks and Tier-3 PT ad rates; treat them as planning constraints, not validated unit economics.

Honest split: **competitive threat and commercial regulation ≈ High; market sizing and GTM mechanics ≈ Low–Medium; monetisation of the locals thesis ≈ Low / unevidenced.** Strategy that assumes consumer ARPU is validated, or that creator-sold lists are white space, is standing on thin ice.

## 3. Highest confidence findings

1. **TheFork Feed is already live in Portugal** (from 15 Jul 2025) — not a future risk; social lists + book-from-list inside a booking incumbent.
2. **Uber Eats chef/creator collections launched in Portugal on 9 July 2026** — named PT creators; free consumer curation attached to delivery.
3. **Creator-sold restaurant lists are not NomNom’s invention** — Beli Paid Guides confirmed on Tier 1 surfaces; WoM/Rexby are adjacent paid-curation proofs.
4. **VAT deemed-supplier applies** if NomNom sets T&Cs, authorises charge, and controls delivery — VAT on full consumer price from first sale.
5. **€5/month as the primary consumer subscription is not defensible** for the modal 23–32 Lisbon diner given income, discretionary math, subscription fatigue, and free substitutes.
6. **Paid acquisition is closed for this LTV shape** — even with noisy CPM inputs, CAC and LTV do not meet; unpaid/creator distribution is mandatory.
7. **Fake “62% saves never revisited” and Google Maps “300 + random eviction” must not be cited** — PROGRESS-corrected; pitch-credibility risk.

## 5. Critical unknowns

Things that would change strategy if answered the other way:

1. **Portuguese (especially resident) WTP for a curated restaurant list** at any price — Snapshot vs sub vs neither. `raw/customer-voice.md` exists (Tier-3 proxies) but **no PT diner interviews**. If WTP is ~0 for locals, consumer SKUs collapse to tourist Snapshot side-channel or must move to transaction/B2B.
2. **Founder WTP experiment not yet run** — market synthesis explicitly calls for a cheap intent test (e.g. ~€50 Meta → pricing-page); until results exist, Phase 7 consumer revenue stays Stage A assumption. Outcome could kill or reprice Snapshot/sub before product investment.
3. **Locals vs tourists (Phase 4 fork)** — brand wants locals; every priced precedent and a large German-language “near me” search slice point at visitors. Wrong call poisons graph or starves revenue.
4. **TheFork Feed / Uber Top Eats actual PT usage and creator lock-in** — shipped ≠ adopted; if Feed is already habitual, wedge narrows further.
5. **Lisbon creator supply census (8k–60k local engagement)** — cold start assumes tens of partners; pool may be thinner after Uber signing.
6. **Whether single-player day-one utility can be shipped** — Beli lesson vs multiplayer emptiness; false negative on product if graph is empty for user #1.
7. **Stacked waterfall viability** — VAT on gross + store cut + creator share + NomNom take; may leave creators worse off than one Reel.
8. **Beachhead freguesia verification** — wrong cluster → empty graph → false PMF negative.
9. **Founder inputs still open in brief** — model intent, prices, runway, live traction, prior customer conversations (gates Phase 3.7 and financial realism).

## 6. Recommendations

Ordered by how much they de-risk strategy:

1. **Run a founder WTP experiment first (this week)** — landing page with Snapshot (€3.99–7.99) and optional low sub (€2.99–3.99/mo); ~€50 Meta or organic creator traffic; measure CTR → email/checkout intent. Do **not** treat desk-research ARPU as validated until this returns.
2. **Close or explicitly defer primary interviews** — run 8–12 Lisbon resident interviews (Inês/Tiago JTBD, pay/not-pay, list-share behaviour) **or** document deferral of Phase 3.7 with eyes open. Desk `customer-voice.md` alone does not close WTP.
3. **Install-and-document Beli Paid Guides + spot-check TheFork Feed PT** — prices, creator eligibility, whether Feed surfaces in a PT account; kill any remaining novelty language.
4. **Tax adviser before first euro** (~€2–5k) — VAT OSS / deemed supplier + creator withholding; model waterfall before promising creator take rates.
5. **Verify Arroios / Campo de Ourique / Alvalade demographics** from INE/freguesia tables before locking acquisition geography.
6. **Hand-audit 30–60 Lisbon food creators** (follower quality, Uber Top Eats overlap) before promising a 40-creator cold start.
7. **Decide locals vs tourists in Phase 4 in writing** — product filters and SKU architecture; Snapshot-as-tourist cash vs resident graph must not be accidental.
8. **Ship web-first paid content; apps read/consume** — until store+VAT math is modelled; keep paid CAC at €0 for ≥90 days.
9. **Re-architect Google Maps import to `place_id` only** before list scale — ToS termination risk is feature-fatal.
