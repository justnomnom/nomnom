### "Why Now" Narrative

**Timing thesis:** Restaurant discovery is shifting from star-average portals toward (a) AI-synthesized shortlists that homogenise choice and amplify review gaming, and (b) social/creator trust graphs. Hyper-tourist cities like Lisbon raise the cost of bad recommendations, creating demand for attributable local taste — delivered in the channels people already use (WhatsApp, IG, Maps saves).

### What NOT to Do

#### DoorDash Zesty shutdown (Apr 2026) — lessons
- **[Data]** DoorDash wound down standalone AI discovery app **Zesty** (~Dec 2025 launch → dark **17 Apr 2026**); folded personalized recs + conversational search into core DoorDash app (Nation’s Restaurant News; Restaurant Technology News; AL.com).
- Lessons for investors/founders (**[Opinion]** industry analysis MetaAdvisor / LinkedIn commentary):
  1. Discovery as a *separate app* fails when the transaction (or habit) lives elsewhere.
  2. AI conversational search alone is not differentiation — baseline feature.
  3. Social/photo/review layers get dropped when reintegrated into commerce apps.
- **Implication for NomNom:** Do not pitch “standalone AI discovery app.” Pitch **trust + distribution surface** (auth-free public list links) that incumbents don’t own. Distinguish clearly from DoorDash Zesty *and* from Zest (Plaid).

#### Cream (AU) — discovery WTP failure
- **[Data]** Founder Tom Nijam post-mortem: after ~24 months, shut Cream. Core line: *“Discovering the best place to eat isn’t an acute problem, it’s a nice-to-have, and so diners will never pay us to access this information.”*
- Secondary lessons (SWLH “Lessons Learned From Cream”): diner-side freemium needs scale advertising or venue B2B; hospitality venue TAM math often doesn’t support venture outcomes; EGC slower than UGC for virality.
- **Implication:** Do not pitch consumer subscription for “better restaurant lists” as primary model without extraordinary proof. Prefer creator monetization, B2B, or take-rate on something scarce.

#### abillion (2026) — discovery layer under AI + fundraising winter
- **[Data]** Vegan discovery/reviews platform shut ~Mar 2026 despite ~2M active users (Dec 2025) and >$17M raised historically; blamed fundraising environment (Failory / Vegan-News). Commentary: AI eating standalone curation.
- **Implication:** Scale without monetization/moat is not a story; “mission + reviews” is fragile.

### Implications for NomNom Pitch

1. **Lead narrative:** Trusted people (friends + creators) → places, delivered via **public list link without install**, starting in **Lisbon** where tourist-trap noise is culturally legible.
2. **Steal structure, not claims:** Beli’s trust-over-stars; Rex/Accel’s “frictionless capture of real taste”; Mapstr’s community love; WoM’s independence — but **own** zero-install EU distribution.
3. **Why now:** AI homogenisation + fake-review fatigue + Lisbon authenticity demand — phrased as *human-attributable recommendations in the chat layer*, not “we’re AI.”
4. **Monetization honesty:** Study Beli’s still-open revenue questions and Cream’s WTP failure; if Paid Guides / creator commerce is the model, say so early.
5. **Competitive humility:** Mapstr/Beli/WoM/Zest prove category interest; differentiation = channel + city + trust provenance, not “first lists.”
6. **Anti-patterns:** No DoorDash-Zesty-style “AI discovery app”; no Uber/ChatGPT-for-food; no fabricated Maps/save statistics; no invented pitch decks as proof.

## Flags

### Red Flags
- Pitching NomNom as inventing creator lists or social restaurant ranking.
- Leading with AI-native / ChatGPT-for-dining identity in mid-2026.
- Consumer-pay-for-discovery as sole business model without evidence (Cream).
- Confusing **Zest** (Plaid, 776/Kindred) with **Zesty** (DoorDash, shut Apr 2026).
- Using unverified viral stats (Maps “300 evictions,” “62% saves never revisited”).
- Citing DoorDash YC deck as a social-discovery template without caveats.

### Yellow Flags
- “Beli for Europe” without answering “why won’t Beli expand?”
- Heavy tourist-trap rhetoric that sounds anti-visitor to hospitality investors.
- Link-first / zero-install claim without metrics (opens → saves → return visits).
- Portugal raise comps skewed to B2B (BRAINR, Feedzai) — consumer discovery raises are thinner locally; don’t overclaim “Portugal loves consumer food apps.”
- World of Mouth funding amounts opaque — don’t cite as large VC validation.
- Mapstr Crowdcube success ≠ traditional VC Series A narrative.
