## Competitive Framing for Pitch

### How Competitors Position to Investors

#### Beli (US) — friends-first ranking / “social list-keeping”
- **Raised:** **[Data]** $5.3M Seed/Series A led by Goodwater Capital (announced ~Nov 2023; CB Insights dates Seed VC 29 Nov 2023). Founders later cited ~$12M total raised as of mid-2025 (SF Chronicle / Wikipedia synthesis, Jun 2025 coverage). PitchBook/CB Insights totals differ (~$6.15M vs founder-stated $12M) — treat total as **[Estimate]** until primary filing.
- **Investor narrative:** Preference data from friends beats anonymous Yelp/Google reviews; Gen Z utility app (“social restaurant list-keeping,” not a review platform); organic referral growth; lean ops (team of ~4 cited 2025). **[Data]** SF Chronicle (2025); DailyDropout.fyi; Wikipedia Beli page.
- **Monetization story investors hear now:** Paid Guides / creator subscriptions (in-app IAP + Stripe; Beli as limited payments agent for creators) + invite-only Supper Club (NYC). **[Data]** beliapp.com Paid Guides Terms; App Store IAP SKUs (~$5.79/mo creator subs, Supper Club annual). B2B “Insights Suite” is still speculative in secondary analysis **[Opinion/Estimate]** (Ivey Business Review / break-even blogs — not confirmed product launch).
- **What resonated:** Trust graph + ranking habit; anti-ad-feed stance; clear wedge vs Yelp.
- **Where narrative is weak for NomNom’s room:** App-install required; US coastal density; monetization still early/experimental relative to user hype; “we invented social restaurant lists” is crowded. **[Opinion]**

#### Mapstr (FR) — private maps + community capital
- **Raised:** **[Data]** Crowdcube equity crowdfunding ~€1.5M (May 2022; Mapstr about page / VCBacked); CB Insights total raised ~$2.3M including earlier angels. Community ownership is the story.
- **Investor narrative:** “Word-of-mouth 2.0”; French/independent; privacy (no selling data / no in-app ads in crowdfunding pitch); users become shareholders. **[Data]** Mapstr Crowdcube campaign copy (“premier réseau social qui appartient à ses utilisateurs”).
- **What resonated:** Proof of love via crowdfund velocity; multi-category place bookmarking (not only restaurants).
- **Weakness:** Broad “save places” utility, not creator trust graph or walk-in commerce; crowdfund ≠ institutional GTM thesis. **[Opinion]**

#### Rex (US) — Accel seed / camera-roll as moat
- **Raised:** **[Data]** $3.96M seed led by Accel with Khosla Ventures, Future Positive (Biz Stone), Twitter/Snap/Pinterest angels (TechCrunch / Finsmes, 12 Jun 2023).
- **Investor narrative (Accel memo):** People already curate rec lists awkwardly; camera roll is reliable signal of where you’ve been; AI/CV lowers creation friction → shareable “playlists” of places; trust > one-size-fits-all blogs. **[Data]** Accel Noteworthy: “Turning Your Photos Into Curated Recommendations” (Amit Kumar); TechCrunch 12 Jun 2023.
- **What resonated:** Differentiated *creation* wedge (“no one else has attempted camera-roll mining”); founder/team pedigree (Twitter alumni).
- **Weakness:** Install + photo-permission friction; AI-native identity (NomNom should not copy); still consumer social discovery without PT auth-free public-link distribution. **[Opinion]**

#### Zest (US, 2026) — card-linked “where people actually eat” *(≠ DoorDash Zesty)*
- **Raised:** **[Data]** $1.8M pre-seed from Alexis Ohanian’s **776** and Steve Jang’s **Kindred Ventures** (TechCrunch 10 Jun 2026). Founded Nov 2024; public launch ~May 2026; “100,000+ visits” post-launch claimed in coverage **[Estimate]** (visits ≠ DAU).
- **Investor narrative:** Reviews/IG are aspirational; Plaid transaction data is ground truth; anti social-posturing; follow friends’ real dining maps. **[Data]** TechCrunch; App Store copy.
- **What resonated:** Clear data moat thesis for US VCs; category still fundable at pre-seed in 2026.
- **Weakness:** High privacy/friction (card link); US payment rails (Plaid); monetization path unclear in press; naming collision with failed DoorDash **Zesty**. **[Opinion]**

#### TheFork (EU, PT live) — booking platform → social Feed → Amex acquisition
- **Corporate event:** **[Data]** American Express announced proposed acquisition of TheFork from Tripadvisor for **$700M cash** (~€604M) on **15 Jun 2026**; expected close before end-2026 subject to regs/labor consultation (Amex IR; Observador PT).
- **Feed narrative (product → investors/operators):** Word-of-mouth productized inside booking app — follow friends/chefs/influencers; favourite lists; book from lists; only bookers can review (authenticity claim). Feed launched **15 Jul 2025** (TheFork Engineering Blog). PT press positioned TheFork as first “social network” for restaurants. **[Data]** theforkmanager.com Feed launch; EchoBoomer PT; Grande Consumo.
- **Investor/strategic thesis (Amex):** Dining as card engagement; European inventory (~50k restaurants / 11 countries incl. Portugal) complements Resy/Tock → ~75k venues combined **[Estimate]** (Amex announcement methodologies).
- **Pitch implication:** Incumbents will claim “social recommendations” loudly — but discovery is a **funnel to reservation + card loyalty**, not a creator/friend list product for walk-ins. **[Opinion]**

#### Uber Eats Top Eats (Portugal, Jul 2026) — chef/creator curation inside delivery
- **Launch:** **[Data]** Uber Eats Portugal launched **Top Eats** ~9 Jul 2026: chefs (Avillez, Justa Nobre, Kiko Martins, etc.) + food creators (A Pitada do Pai, Aqui há Garfo, Gastropiço, etc.) recommend dishes/restaurants with in-app badge; podcast/content layer. Framing: evolve from convenience to “motor de descoberta gastronómica.” **[Data]** ECO; Marketeer; Trendy.pt; Distribuição Hoje.
- **Investor/strategic narrative:** Use celebrity/creator trust to reduce choice overload **inside delivery inventory**.
- **Pitch implication:** Validates creator curation demand in PT **and** shows big platforms will co-opt it for delivery GMV — not for auth-free walk-in trust lists. **[Opinion]**

#### World of Mouth (FI) — expert network + paid independence
- **Raised:** **[Data/Estimate]** Tracxn/CB Insights: funded, 1 institutional investor; **amount undisclosed**. Not a classic VC scale narrative.
- **Narrative:** Independent guide powered by 750–800+ chefs/writers/sommeliers; no ads / no sponsored lists; membership (€9.90/mo or ~€3.90/mo annual) funds independence; AI “Basil” trained on expert corpus. Claims ~160k users / 25k+ recs (LinkedIn manifesto update). **[Data]** worldofmouth.app; WoM LinkedIn; App Store.
- **Weakness for NomNom contrast:** Expert-elite brand, not friends+local creators; subscription-for-access; global city coverage ambition over Lisbon density. Explicitly anti-sponsored — opposite of Snapshot (must frame Snapshot as *marked*, not opaque ads). **[Opinion]**

#### Bestie Bite (IT) — video reviews + AI venue marketing *(Wave 1 EU comp)*
- **Raised:** **[Data]** Total ~€2.2M by Jun 2026: ~€700k Techstars-led then €1.5M SAFE led by G&G (Grassi family) for US expansion (EU-Startups; Finsmes; TechFundingNews, Jun 2026). Earlier Web Summit 2024 $500k pre-seed open (press release).
- **Narrative:** Short authentic videos beat TripAdvisor text; dual-sided — consumer discovery + “AI Marketing Employee” for restaurants (WhatsApp-managed content ops mentioned in Tech.eu Dec 2025).
- **Pitch use:** Shows Southern EU hospitality discovery can raise mid-pre-seed; their wedge is **video UGC + venue SaaS**, not trust-graph lists. **[Opinion]**

#### Bloop (PT) — social shopping *(adjacent, not direct)*
- **Raised:** **[Data]** ~€1.4–1.5M pre-seed (Monarque + crowdfunding; JE/Monarque 2025); later press cites ~€2M / 150+ investors and Spain expansion (ECO Jun 2026).
- **Narrative:** “First social shopping network”; recommend-to-earn credits; experiences/restaurants as future SKU, not core today.
- **Pitch use:** Useful as **PT capital comps**, not product comps — avoid implying restaurant-list category is “solved” by Bloop. **[Opinion]**

#### Barhio (Barcelona) — near-identical wedge (critical watch)
- **Status:** **[Data]** Public site: “Now launching in Barcelona”; friend ratings only; zero ads; free forever; save from IG; import Google Maps; **share via WhatsApp — friends see a map, no app install needed**. **[DATA GAP]** No verified funding round found in Tracxn/growth lists as of research date.
- **Pitch implication:** Barhio proves the zero-install shareable-list narrative is **already being claimed in Iberia** (their site: share via WhatsApp). NomNom cannot pitch “we invented no-install list links” — must own **Lisbon density + creators+friends + Snapshot commerce for walk-ins** instead. **[Opinion]**

#### DoorDash Zesty (US) — failed standalone AI discovery *(negative comp)*
- **Event:** **[Data]** DoorDash wound down Zesty ~Apr 2026 (<6 months after Dec 2025 launch in NYC/SF); folding personalized recs / conversational search into core DoorDash app (NRN 6 Apr 2026; Restaurant Technology News).
- **Lesson for pitch:** Standalone “AI restaurant discovery” without a distribution wedge dies or gets absorbed; investors have fresh scar tissue. NomNom should **not** lead with AI-native identity. Frame as trust-graph + auth-free public-link distribution; AI (if any) is secondary. **[Opinion]**

#### DIG-IN / Google Maps / IG Saved *(default alternatives)*
- **DIG-IN (ex-Zomato PT):** **[Data]** App Store claims >2M monthly views in Portugal; menus, reserve, pay, rate, delivery — mass discovery directory, not friend trust graph.
- **Google Maps / Instagram Saved:** Default behavior; no creator commerce layer; lists fragmented across screenshots. Competitors (Beli, Mapstr, Barhio, Rex) all pitch against this status quo. **[Assumption]** still primary “do nothing” competitor for NomNom users.

### Narrative Gaps to Exploit
*Ranked by pitch impact for €200k PT pre-seed (Bynd / Shilling / Portugal Ventures lens).*

1. **Auth-free / zero-install public lists as primary distribution (PT-native)**  
   - **Why competitors can’t fully claim this:** Beli, Rex, Zest, World of Mouth, DIG-IN, TheFork Feed, Uber Top Eats all require their app. Mapstr is install-first. Barhio *does* claim WhatsApp no-install share — but for Barcelona launch, not Lisbon density. **[Data]** Barhio site; Marktest 2025: WhatsApp highest social penetration in PT at **89.9%** (Meios&Publicidade / ECO, Sep 2025) — channel context for where links travel.  
   - **How to frame:** “Lists travel as a public link — no install — into the chats where Portuguese already coordinate dinner.”  
   - **Evidence:** Marktest penetration (channel); Barhio as proof the wedge works *and* that NomNom must localize the claim to Lisbon creators+friends, not “category invention.”

2. **Walk-in / Snapshot commerce vs booking & delivery funnels**  
   - **Why competitors can’t claim this:** TheFork’s social layer converts to **reservations**; Uber Top Eats converts to **delivery GMV**; Amex buys TheFork for **card engagement**. None solve marked, one-time sponsorship for tasca/walk-in venues that don’t live in booking/delivery inventory. **[Data]** Amex IR 15 Jun 2026; Uber ECO 9 Jul 2026; TheFork Feed blog.  
   - **How to frame:** “Incumbents monetize the bookable/deliverable half of Lisbon. Snapshot is how trusted lists pay for the walk-in half — labeled, not stealth ads.”  
   - **Evidence:** Category structure (booking vs delivery vs lists); Snapshot as product thesis **[Assumption]** pending NomNom traction proof.

3. **Lisbon authenticity / city-density wedge (not global “Instagram for restaurants”)**  
   - **Why competitors can’t claim this:** US comps (Beli/Rex/Zest) sell national/global consumer social; World of Mouth sells expert global guide; Uber/TheFork sell platform inventory. City-first density is how Mapstr/Barhio-style products win locally — NomNom can own Lisbon with creators locals actually trust. **[Opinion]**  
   - **How to frame:** “Win Lisbon’s trust graph first — creators + friends — then expand. Not another AI feed fighting Google.”  
   - **Evidence:** Wave 1 audience preference for distribution insight; Uber Top Eats using PT chef names shows local trust sells.

4. **Marked sponsorship honesty vs “no ads forever” absolutism**  
   - **Gap:** World of Mouth / Barhio brand on *zero ads*; Beli avoids in-feed ads but sells Paid Guides; opaque IG sponsorship is the status quo users distrust. Snapshot as **explicit, one-time, labeled** placement is a third path. **[Opinion]**  
   - **Frame carefully:** Do not attack “independence” brands by sounding payola — lead with disclosure UX and creator control.

5. **Avoid claiming invention of “creator lists”**  
   - TheFork Feed, Uber Top Eats, Beli Paid Guides, Spotli.st, Lets Discover (London), ByChefs Lisbon all already occupy creator/chef curation language (2025–26). **[Data]** sources above; ByChefs Lisbon rankings live (lisbon.bychefs.com; Lima Complimenta coverage).  
   - **Exploit instead:** combination of **friends + local creators + zero-install link + walk-in Snapshot** — the stack, not any single noun.

### Competitive Positioning One-Liner

- **Candidate 1:** “NomNom is the auth-free trust graph for Lisbon dining — creator and friend lists that work as a public link, with marked Snapshots for walk-in venues booking apps ignore.” — Strength: **H** (distribution + city + monetization + anti-incumbent).  
- **Candidate 2:** “Beli for Lisbon, without the install — lists that travel as a public link, paid by honest Snapshots not fake reviews.” — Strength: **M** (clear comp, but “Beli for X” invites clone discount; Barhio collision).  
- **Candidate 3:** “TheFork and Uber now curate inside their apps; NomNom curates the dinner decision via a shareable list before anyone opens an app.” — Strength: **M–H** (sharp vs Jul 2026 news; weaker on monetization).  
- **Candidate 4:** “Trusted restaurant lists from creators and friends — zero install, Lisbon-first, Snapshot-funded.” — Strength: **M** (clean; less competitive tension).  

**Recommended:** **Candidate 1** — it encodes the three gaps PT angels care about (auth-free distribution, Lisbon authenticity, walk-in Snapshot) and preempts TheFork/Uber without claiming “we invented creator lists” or an AI-native identity.

> **Confidence:** **Medium–High** on competitor fundraise/positioning facts (multi-source for Beli/Rex/Zest/TheFork/Uber/Zesty); **Medium** on Barhio trajectory (product claims public, funding opaque); **Medium** on Snapshot as investable wedge (product thesis, pre-traction).  
> Data labels applied throughout. Do not present Estimates/Assumptions as closed books in the spoken pitch without caveats.

## Flags

### Red Flags
- **Barhio feature overlap:** WhatsApp share without install + friend ratings + IG/Maps import is publicly claimed in Barcelona (Jul 2026 site). Pitch must not claim exclusive invention of this mechanic.  
- **Pre-traction vs funded comps:** Zest ($1.8M), Bestie Bite (€2.2M), Beli (~$5.3M+) raised with stronger narrative proof or dual-sided traction stories. €200k ask needs ruthless milestone clarity.  
- **Incumbent narrative capture:** TheFork Feed + Uber Top Eats + Amex $700M make “social dining discovery” sound occupied unless NomNom sharply separates walk-in / auth-free share.

### Yellow Flags
- **Beli total funding inconsistency** ($5.3M round vs ~$12M founder-stated total) — don’t cite $12M without caveating.  
- **Zest visit metrics** (“100k visits”) are vanity-adjacent — don’t mirror that language for NomNom.  
- **World of Mouth “no sponsored lists”** ideology may color angel questions about Snapshot — prepare disclosure demo.  
- **ByChefs Lisbon** (chef-voted rankings) + DIG-IN directory density = local noise; keep NomNom about *people you know*, not another ranking brand.  
- **Solo founder discount** (Wave 1) compounds crowded-market objection — pair competitive frame with distribution insight and hiring plan.
