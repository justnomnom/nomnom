# Full Pitch Narrative: NomNom
*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 26 July 2026*

## Pitch DNA
- **Lead element:** Insight (distribution) — pre-traction
- **2-sentence opener:** NomNom helps people in Lisbon decide where to eat from spots that creators and friends they trust actually saved — not anonymous Google stars. Lists, profiles, and Roulette open from a public link — no app install required.
- **Unique insight:** In this category, product features do not decide winners — distribution does. DoorDash’s Zesty died in months with unlimited budget because discovery habits already live inside Google, Instagram, and group chats. The company that wins Lisbon shows up as a creator’s public list someone opens, not as another app to download.
- **Ordering rationale:** No revenue/MAU to lead with; team bio incomplete; market is modest — so lead with the non-obvious distribution thesis, then prove the product already exists for that thesis.
- **Target audience:** PT angels + pre-seed (Bynd / Shilling / Portugal Ventures band)
- **Ask:** €200k pre-seed **[Assumption]** for 18 months Lisbon beachhead + WTP proof

## Opening (~45 seconds)

NomNom helps people in Lisbon decide where to eat from spots that creators and friends they trust actually saved — not anonymous Google stars. Lists, profiles, and Roulette open from a public link — no app install required.

**Example:** A group stuck on “onde jantar?” opens one list from a creator they follow, shortlists three places in their neighbourhood, spins NomNom Roulette once — and nobody had to download anything.

**Speaker notes:** Pause after the example. Invite: “Does that sound familiar from any Lisbon group chat?” Do not say “Portuguese Beli.”

**Transition:** “Here’s the non-obvious part — why another restaurant product can still make sense.”

## 1. Unique Insight (~90 seconds)

The graveyard in restaurant discovery is full of *good products*. DoorDash launched Zesty in late 2025 and shut it down by April 2026. The lesson was not “people don’t want better recommendations.” It was: **standalone discovery without a distribution wedge cannot beat the apps people already open.**

So NomNom is not pitching “we invented creator lists.” Beli already sells Paid Guides in the US. TheFork Feed (July 2025) and Uber Eats Top Eats in Portugal (9 July 2026) already ship creator curation *inside* booking and delivery.

What they do **not** own:

1. **Auth-free share** — a trusted list that opens without forcing an install before the group can use it.
2. **Walk-in Lisbon** — tascas and non-delivery venues that booking/delivery rails structurally under-serve.
3. **Paying the local curator** (not just giving them a badge) for that list.

**Insight in one line:** Arrive as a trusted public list people can open and decide from — and treat install as an upgrade, not the entry price.

**Speaker notes:** Pause. Let them push on Uber/TheFork. Do not get defensive — concede validation, then restate the three gaps.

**Transition:** “That insight only matters if the pain is real. Here’s the problem.”

---

## 2. Problem (~60 seconds)

Saving is solved. Returning — and deciding with friends — is not.

Inês (mid-20s, Lisbon) discovers restaurants on Reels, dumps them into Instagram Saved or a Google Maps list, then weeks later the group chat becomes a veto loop: ten screenshots, no consensus, someone picks a tourist trap out of fatigue.

The stack being replaced is not “Tripadvisor.” It is **Instagram Saved + Google Maps lists + screenshots + messy group-chat threads**.

**Speaker notes:** One concrete vignette only. Do not cite the fake “62% never revisit saves” figure. Do not claim Google Maps “randomly evicts” at 300 items — unverified.

**Transition:** “Here’s what a user actually does in NomNom.”

---

## 3. Solution — show, don’t tell (~90 seconds)

**What they see:**

1. Someone opens a NomNom public list link — no account required to view.
2. Places are real venues with **named human provenance** (which creator or friend saved them), not a star average.
3. The group filters by neighbourhood / occasion, or spins **Roulette** to kill deadlock.
4. Power users later install for personal archives, maps, and follows — optional.

**What already ships [Data, product]:** Public `/lists`, `/u/handle` profiles, Roulette (`/roleta/lisboa`), map, Google Maps import, PT/EN, web + Capacitor shells — built to v5.7.0 in ~four months (~613 commits since March 2026).

**What we do *not* claim:** Live paying customers, creator GMV, or that payments are production-on for the marketplace. Monetisation is designed; demand is unproven. Year 1 job is density + share-loop proof, not revenue theatre.

**Speaker notes:** If you can demo a live public list on your phone, do it silently for 20 seconds. Demo > adjectives.

**Transition:** “Why this moment — briefly.”

---

## 4. Why Now (~45 seconds — support, not hero)

In one year, AI went from niche to how **45%** of consumers get local business recommendations **[Data, BrightLocal LCRS 2026]** — but AI surfaces a tiny winners’ circle: ~**83%** of restaurant locations never appear in AI recommendations **[Data, Uberall May 2026]**; the top **10%** of restaurants capture ~**75%** of AI visibility **[Data, Local Falcon May 2026]**.

In Lisbon that sameness collides with tourist-trap fatigue. Booking and delivery giants are bolting on creator badges — validating trust discovery while leaving **auth-free lists for walk-in venues** under-served.

**Speaker notes:** One AI stat only. Do not lead with “fake reviews.” BrightLocal still shows ~49% trust reviews as much as personal recs.

**Transition:** “How big is this, honestly.”

## 6. Business Model (~45 seconds)

**Priority order [Assumption + research]:**

1. **Marked sponsored / restaurant-side** — fixed fee, radical disclosure (pool 5–20× consumer subs).
2. **Snapshot** — one-time list purchase (~€3.99–7.99) — primary *consumer* SKU to test; trip/tourist context first.
3. **Creator subscriptions** — experiment only; ≤~€2.99–3.99/mo if tested. €5/mo is wrong for modal Lisbon discretionary budgets.

**Explicit non-model Year 1:** Paid user acquisition. Estimated CAC per payer ~€238 vs LTV €5–15 — closed **[Estimate]**. Growth = creators + organic share only.

**Speaker notes:** Own Cream’s lesson: diners rarely pay for discovery alone — so consumer free + creator/venue pay.

**Transition:** “Where we are today.”

## 7. Traction — honest (~30 seconds)

**Today:** Product-built, demand-unproven. No disclosed MAU or revenue in this pitch. Shipping velocity is the signal: full Portugal-native stack live enough to run the distribution experiment *this quarter*.

**Next proof points we will buy with this round:** creator pages claimed, public-list share CTR, cluster MAU, first paid Snapshot or marked sponsorship euro — or kill/pivot per written criteria.

**Speaker notes:** Never invent waitlist numbers. If you have any real metric by pitch day, put it here with a timeframe.

**Transition:** “Who’s building this.”

## Red Flags
- Traction, founder bio, and exact raise are **[Assumption]** until founder confirms — do not send externally with placeholders.
- Validation scorecard (~5/10) says this is a time-boxed experiment, not a scale fantasy — pitching venture-scale PT consumer will fail diligence.
- Creator-sold lists and zero-install share are not novel (Beli; Barhio Barcelona).

## Yellow Flags
- €200k may sit below Bynd’s €250–600k first-ticket floor — angel-led or club/upsize.
- Solo founder discount without GTM plan.
- Sponsored revenue can poison trust if disclosure is sloppy.
- Local WTP for lists unproven; Snapshot may be tourist-skewed early.
