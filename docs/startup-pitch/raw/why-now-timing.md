## Why Now: Timing Thesis

### Primary Timing Driver

- **What changed:** Local restaurant discovery is tipping from Google/review-site browsing toward **AI-mediated shortlists** that recommend a tiny, repeated set of places — while consumers still do not fully trust anonymous reviews *or* AI alone.
- **When it changed:** Concentrated **2025 → mid-2026**.
  - BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey **2026**: consumers using AI tools for local business recommendations rose from **6% (2025 survey)** to **45%** — becoming the **third** most-used recommendation source behind Google and Facebook, ahead of Yelp/TripAdvisor. **[Data]** BrightLocal LCRS 2026 / BrightLocal “Half of consumers are asking AI…” page.
  - Concurrently, Google as a review/recommendation source slipped **83% → 71%** (same BrightLocal year-over-year framing, widely syndicated). **[Data]** BrightLocal LCRS 2026 via BrightLocal + KitchenRush aggregation.
- **Evidence of homogenisation / concentration (verified form — not “same 5 spots”):**
  1. **Uberall Fast Food, Faster Discovery / 2026 GEO Playbook for Multi-Location QSRs** (press: BusinessWire **8 May 2026**; Uberall site): **83%** of restaurant locations are **entirely invisible** in AI-generated recommendations; only **~17%** ever appear when consumers ask ChatGPT-style tools where to eat (despite ~86% having some Google presence). Also cites **~1.2%** of all local businesses surfaced by ChatGPT (SOCi Local Visibility Index, widely reported). **[Data]** Uberall + BusinessWire; SOCi via National Law Review syndication.
  2. **Local Falcon Restaurant AI Visibility Index** (analysis of **10,000 US restaurants**, **May 2026**; TechNewsWorld coverage): **74.9%** of restaurants invisible in Google AI recommendations; **top 10% of restaurants capture 74.5% of all AI visibility** (vs **54%** on Google Maps). Local Falcon CEO quote: diners get “a short, often repetitive list.” **[Data]** Local Falcon + TechNewsWorld.
- **Impact on NomNom:** The credible “why now” is **not** “reviews are dead” (they’re not — see below). It is: **AI collapses choice into a winners’ circle**, so attributable human taste (friends + trusted creators) becomes more valuable *exactly as* AI shortlists go mainstream — especially in a city where tourist-trap / homogenised lists already feel culturally painful (Lisbon).

> **Correction vs Wave 1 phrasing:** Do **not** pitch BrightLocal/KitchenRush as proving “AI always recommends the same 5 spots.” Pitch **concentration + invisibility** (Uberall/Local Falcon/SOCi), optionally with Local Falcon’s “repetitive list” language. KitchenRush is a **Tier-3 aggregator/vendor blog**, not the primary source.

### Supporting Timing Signals

1. **WhatsApp as default social infrastructure in Portugal (distribution timing)**  
   - Marktest “Os Portugueses e as Redes Sociais **2025**” (fieldwork **3–15 July 2025**, n=801, ages 15–64): WhatsApp first in **penetration** among social-network users at **89.9%** (first time #1 in that study); Instagram leads frequency of use (~32% mentions); WhatsApp ~25% frequency mentions. Covered by ECO (18 Sep 2025), Telecompaper (24 Sep 2025), Wintech, Grande Consumo. **[Data]** Marktest via ECO/Telecompaper.  
   - **Strength: Strong** for Portugal-first GTM and “meet users in the chat layer” — not for global WhatsApp commerce claims.  
   - Separate 2025–26 **investor narrative** (mostly B2B): Wassist **$1.1M** pre-seed led by Playfair (**June 2026**) — “WhatsApp is where conversations already are” / commerce agents. TextYess **€2.4M** (WhatsApp e-commerce, Milan). **[Data]** TechFundingNews / Wassist / Silicon Canals.  
   - **Strength for NomNom raise narrative: Moderate** — proves VCs fund chat/link-native *distribution*, but comps are **commerce agents**, not consumer restaurant discovery. Adapt carefully: “link-native / zero-install,” not “we’re Wassist for food.”

2. **Incumbents validating trust/creator discovery in Europe & Portugal (category inflection — also a threat)**  
   - **TheFork Feed** launched **15 July 2025** (TheFork Engineering Medium; LinkedIn Alberto Conti): follow friends/creators, lists, social activity → book. CEO Almir Ambeskovic cited **~58%** ask friends for restaurant recommendations; other TheFork materials cite **>80%** word-of-mouth reliance in FR/IT. Live across TheFork’s EU footprint (11 countries). **[Data]** TheFork Manager blog (21 Aug 2025 post), Medium eng blog, Grande Consumo / Il Sole 24 Ore.  
   - **Uber Eats Top Eats Portugal** launched **9 July 2026** (ECO, Marketeer, EchoBoomer, Grande Consumo): curation by chefs (Avillez, Justa Nobre, Kiko Martins, etc.) **and** food creators (A Pitada do Pai, Aqui há Garfo, Gastropiço, O Comensal, Pedro o Bom Garfo, Teresa Castro Viana); “discovery engine” framing beyond pure convenience. **[Data]** ECO 9 Jul 2026 + peers.  
   - **Strength: Strong as market validation; Moderate-as-threat** — proves the thesis is live in PT, but also that **delivery + booking giants are already shipping creator/trust layers**. NomNom’s wedge must be **auth-free public links + friend/creator provenance outside pay-to-play delivery/booking rails**, not “we invented curated lists.”

3. **Lisbon authenticity / anti-tourist-trap cultural pressure (why *here*)**  
   - Ongoing media: CN Traveller on tascas under tourism pressure; Irish Independent / Bloomberg lineage on **movimento taberneiro**; Taste of Lisboa anti-trap guides; Roads & Kingdoms **2026** “Young Chefs Saving Lisbon’s Tascas”; Idealista **10 Mar 2026** on traditional café closures; Eater on pastel-de-nata as overtourism symbol. **[Data]** qualitative Tier-2/3 media (not a single “2026 inflection” datapoint).  
   - **Strength: Strong for city choice / problem vividness; Weak as a standalone global “why now” clock** — the cultural tension is multi-year (Bloomberg piece **Apr 2023** already named movimento taberneiro). Use as **why Lisbon first**, not as proof the market suddenly opened in 2026.

4. **Social discovery category took off (Beli adoption curve) — precedent that friends > stars is fundable**  
   - Beli App Store **Jun 2021**; campus/Word-of-mouth acceleration **~2023**; Food Network (2025): **13M** ratings in 2023 → **30M** in 2024 → **25M+** YTD at time of article; Startup Signals: **~58M** ratings by **Q2 2025**; SF Chronicle / Wikipedia: company states **~$12M** raised, **80%** users under 35, organic growth. **[Data]** Food Network, Taste Cooking, SF Chronicle, Wikipedia/DailyDropout.  
   - **Strength: Strong** as category timing (trust-graph dining is no longer sci-fi); **Weak** as NomNom-specific urgency (Beli already exists; ~10% international users — EU still open, but “wait for Beli” is a real objection).

5. **DoorDash Zesty shutdown (Apr 2026) — timing signal with a double edge**  
   - Zesty launched **Dec 2025** (NY/SF); wound down; app dark **17 Apr 2026**; personalized recs + conversational search folded into core DoorDash; social feed apparently **not** carried over (Nation’s Restaurant News **6 Apr 2026**; Restaurant Technology News; AL.com). **[Data]** NRN + peers.  
   - **Why-now reading:** Standalone AI discovery apps are failing; incumbents pull AI into **transaction** apps — leaving room for products that own **trust provenance + chat distribution**, not another AI chat UI.  
   - **Too-late reading:** Giants are absorbing discovery into delivery/booking; pure discovery is a feature, not a company.  
   - **Strength: Moderate** — best used as **anti-pattern proof** (“we are not Zesty”), not as the primary clock.

6. **Fake-review / trust climate — use carefully**  
   - BrightLocal LCRS **2026**: still **49%** of consumers trust online reviews **as much as** personal recommendations; **97%** still use reviews to guide decisions; **97%** want punishment for fake-review abuse; FTC fake-review rule context (US, 2024+) widely cited. **[Data]** BrightLocal.  
   - **Pitch rule:** Prefer **AI-sameness/concentration** over “reviews are fake / trust collapsed.” Leading with fake reviews invites the rebuttal that half still trust reviews as personal recs.

7. **Market-size reports (low pitch weight)**  
   - MarketIntelo-style figures: restaurant discovery platform market ~**$6.2B (2025)** → ~**$16.8B by 2034** at **11.4% CAGR**; Growth Market Reports: **$5.8B (2024)** → **$19.7B by 2033** at **14.2% CAGR**. **[Estimate / Tier-3 commercial reports]** — useful only as backdrop; do **not** lead a seed pitch with top-down TAM from these vendors.

### "Why Now" Narrative for the Pitch (2 versions + recommended)

**Version 1 (data-led):**  
“In one year, AI went from a niche local-search tool to how **45%** of consumers get business recommendations — but AI surfaces a tiny winners’ circle (**~17%** of restaurants ever appear; top **10%** take ~**75%** of AI visibility). In Lisbon, that sameness collides with tourist-trap fatigue. NomNom puts **friends and trusted creators** back in the loop — via a **public list link** that travels in the chats where **~90%** of Portuguese social users already are (WhatsApp penetration — channel fact) — so discovery stays human and attributable.”

**Version 2 (story-led):**  
“People in Lisbon already ask a friend or a creator where to eat — then lose the tip in WhatsApp chaos while Google and ChatGPT keep sending everyone to the same safe list. Delivery and booking apps are bolting on chef badges and social feeds. We’re building the layer those tips should have been: **trusted people → the right place**, starting in one city, starting in the chat.”

**Recommended:** **Hybrid of V2 lead + one V1 proof line.**  
- Lead with **Lisbon + auth-free public lists + attributable trust** (specific, hard to copy as a slide).  
- Anchor with **one** AI concentration stat (BrightLocal 6%→45% *or* Local Falcon / Uberall — pick one, cite primary).  
- Do **not** lead with a generic “AI is changing everything” deck page — investors are fatigued on that in mid-2026. **[Opinion]**

## Flags

### Red Flags
- Pitching BrightLocal as proving “AI always recommends the same 5 restaurants” without a primary citation (claim not verified; use Local Falcon/Uberall concentration language instead).
- Leading Why Now with “fake reviews killed trust” while BrightLocal still shows **49%** trust reviews ≈ personal recommendations.
- Framing Zesty shutdown as proof NomNom’s market is wide open — it also proves **standalone discovery fails**.
- Claiming NomNom uniquely timed to creator curation while Uber Eats Top Eats (Jul 2026) and TheFork Feed (Jul 2025) already ship it in Europe/PT.
- Using MarketIntelo/Growth Market Reports TAM as the timing thesis.

### Yellow Flags
- KitchenRush / Intermenu tourist-discovery stats are **vendor blogs** (Tier 3) — fine for pointers, not for deck hard numbers.
- Movimento taberneiro / authenticity media is **multi-year**, not a sudden 2026 regulatory or tech unlock.
- WhatsApp-first raise comps (Wassist, TextYess) are **B2B commerce**, not consumer discovery — analogy needs careful framing.
- Beli’s US tipping point can imply NomNom is late *or* that EU is still greenfield — prepare both answers.
- DATA GAP on whether TheFork Feed is deeply used in **Portugal specifically** (TheFork is strong in PT; Feed is EU-wide — local Feed engagement not separately verified here).

## DATA GAPS

1. **Lisbon-specific AI shortlist overlap:** No primary study measuring whether ChatGPT/Gemini recommend the same N restaurants in Lisbon/Alfama/Baixa. Closest proxies are US-centric (Local Falcon) and multi-market QSR (Uberall). **How to fill:** Run a small audit (10 prompts × 3 tools × 3 neighborhoods) before investor meetings; present as founder primary research.
2. **TheFork Feed usage in Portugal:** EU launch confirmed; PT-specific Feed adoption / booking lift **not found**. **How to fill:** TheFork partner stats, App Annie/Sensor Tower, or diner interviews.
3. **NomNom-relevant WhatsApp behavior:** Marktest proves penetration/frequency — **not** that Portuguese users want restaurant discovery *inside* chat apps. **How to fill:** Waitlist conversion from public list shares; retention after link open.
4. **Primary BrightLocal PDF download:** Stats cited via BrightLocal web summaries + reputable syndication; full methodology PDF not re-downloaded in this pass. **How to fill:** Download LCRS 2026 PDF for deck footnote.
5. **KitchenRush “same 5 spots”:** Not verified as BrightLocal primary language. Treat as **unverified slogan**.
6. **Local willingness-to-pay / problem acuity in Lisbon:** No 2025–26 PT survey found on “how painful is tourist-trap discovery for locals vs visitors.” Cream is AU/older. **How to fill:** 15–20 local interviews + visitor intercepts.
7. **Regulatory timing:** EU DSA / fake-review enforcement timing for PT restaurant UGC not deep-dived; FTC is US-centric. Prefer not to hang Why Now on regulation.
