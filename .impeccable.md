# Impeccable — Design Context

_Living document for design skills and UI work. **Product owner:** validate references and a11y targets when you tighten positioning._

## Design Context

### Users

- **Who**: People who want to **eat well anywhere** — locals or travelers; the job is **great food in any place**, not only at home.
- **Product**: **NomNom** — discovery from **people you trust** (creators, locals), not anonymous star averages (`src/config-global.js` `APP.description`).
- **First-session success** (aligned with product): land in a **cozy, fun** flow → location / tastes / creators → **Discover** with picks that feel human.
- **Contexts**: Web (Next.js), **mobile** (safe-area, touch targets), **Capacitor** iOS; **locales** EN + PT.

### Brand Personality

- **Three words**: **Cozy · fun · Nom Nom** — warm, playful, a little hungry; never corporate or judgmental.
- **Voice**: Playful food language, light emoji where it fits onboarding; warm and direct about the value of **trusted** human recommendations.
- **Visual**: **Warm editorial** — terracotta primary (`#FF6B35`), parchment / warm marketing neutrals (`theme.palette.marketing.*`), not cold default gray chrome.
- **Typography**: **Albert Sans** (UI), **Libre Baskerville** (display/emphasis), **JetBrains Mono** (mono) — clear pairing, not one generic face.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Default story: light** — Marketing and core product feel should read as **bright, cozy, daytime-café / editorial magazine** energy first. Dark mode stays supported for preference and contrast, but **light is the brand hero**.
- **Tone**: Cozy + fun over minimal-chic or “premium cold”; **Nom Nom** is the memorable hook (name, CTAs, microcopy).
- **Differentiation**: Human curation and trust vs aggregate scores and generic blue food-app patterns.

### Design Principles

1. **Eat well anywhere** — UX supports changing context (new city, new mood) without feeling like a directory dump.
2. **Cozy & fun** — spacing, copy, and motion feel **welcoming** and **playful**, not stiff or anxious.
3. **Trust is explicit** — reject pay-to-play aesthetics; social proof feels human-sized.
4. **Warmth over generic gray** — use `marketing.*` and tinted neutrals; terracotta as disciplined accent (roughly 60/30/10).
5. **Pair type deliberately** — sans for UI, serif for display; strong hierarchy, readable line length (~65–75ch for long copy).
6. **Mobile-respectful** — safe areas, sensible touch targets; don’t amputate core flows on small screens.
7. **Accessible motion** — respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion explains state, not decoration.
8. **No impeccable “tells”** — no gradient text for headlines; no thick side-stripe borders as fake polish.
