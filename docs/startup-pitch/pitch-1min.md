# 1-Minute Elevator Pitch: NomNom
*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 26 July 2026*

---

## Formal (investor event) — ~150 words

NomNom helps Lisbon diners decide where to eat from spots creators and friends they trust saved — via a public list link, before any app install. The insight: restaurant discovery dies on distribution, not features — Zesty proved that. Giants already do creator badges inside booking and delivery; we own auth-free trusted lists for walk-in Lisbon. Product is built; we’re pre-revenue. Raising €200k pre-seed to seed ~40 creators, prove share and a paid Snapshot or sponsorship path in eighteen months. Happy to send a one-pager.

---

## Casual (networking) — ~140 words

You know when the group can’t pick a restaurant and everyone dumps Instagram screenshots? NomNom is a list from people you actually trust that opens in the browser — no app required. Built it for Lisbon first. Not trying to be another Google Maps. Raising a small pre-seed to get local creators on it and see if the share loop is real. Got a minute next week?

---

## Delivery
- Formal: crisp; stop after ask  
- Casual: smile on “screenshots”; ask for the meeting, not the cheque

---

## Red Flags
Don’t improvise traction numbers in either version

## Yellow Flags
Casual version underplays competition — fine for networking; not for diligence
