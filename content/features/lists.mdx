A useful list is not “Lisbon 2026”. It is **Thursday with friends**, **Alfama
on foot**, **late kitchen**. NomNom Lists are saved, shareable, public or
private. You can follow a list and pick up spots as the owner adds them.

## How they behave

- Create a list, add places from the feed, the map, or search.
- Keep it private while you think; publish when you would defend every pin.
- Follow someone else’s list instead of screenshotting their Story.
- **Import from Google Maps**: paste a public Maps list link. NomNom matches
  places it already has. Names it does not know are **not** invented — they are
  reported so the catalogue can grow honestly.
  Step-by-step: [Export Google Maps saved places into NomNom](/resources/export-google-maps-saved-places).

## Paid lists (creators)

Public lists stay free. If a creator paywalls the rest of a list, diners buy a
[Snapshot](/pricing) (that version, forever) or a Subscription (monthly, all of
their paid lists). You pay the creator. There is no NomNom membership in
between. More in the [creators use case](/use-cases/creators).
