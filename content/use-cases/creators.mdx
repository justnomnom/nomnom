You already do the work: shooting meals, naming neighborhoods, answering “where should we go?” NomNom gives that work a **home**—a profile people can save, lists they can subscribe to, and a **feed** where your spots show up next to other voices they trust.

## What you get, free

- **Publish public lists** with collaborators when you want help curating.
- **One profile link** to your saved places: less “screenshot this,” more credit for the curation you already do.
- **Activity and Discover** so new audiences can find you—without fighting an algorithm for every post.

## When you want to get paid

Two tiers, both opt-in:

- **Snapshot** — readers pay once for permanent access to a specific list as it is today. Great for trip guides and one-off drops.
- **Subscription** — readers pay a monthly fee to follow *you*. It unlocks all of your paid lists and emails them whenever you add or change a restaurant. Great for ongoing curators.

You choose your monthly price and how many restaurants stay free up front. Set up payouts in **Settings → Billing & Payments** to switch any public list to paid; flip it back to free anytime. Payments run through Stripe, NomNom takes a small platform fee, and you can see the breakdown before you publish.

## How diners use you

Followers save your lists, open your profile before a night out, and see **who** recommended each spot—**you**—not a mystery reviewer row. The trust is named.
