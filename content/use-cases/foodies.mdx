You know the loop: open Maps for distance, Instagram for vibes, a blog for a list, and you still do not have a pick. NomNom makes **people you choose** the filter — so every shortcut leads to **context**, not an anonymous average.

## What you get, free

- **Follow locals and creators** you actually like; your Discover feed becomes your shortlist.
- **NomNom lists** for date night, friends, cheap eats, corporate — saved and shareable, public or private.
- **NomNom Roulette** when you are stuck: a random pick from a pool that matches the vibe, not “whatever ranked first.”
- **Maps and search** so you spend less time tab-hopping and more time eating.

## When a list is paid

Some creators paywall part of their list. The first few restaurants stay free; the rest unlock when you either **buy a Snapshot** (one-time, permanent access to that list as it is today) or **subscribe to the creator** (monthly, unlocks all their paid lists and pings you when they add a new spot). You pay the creator directly — there is no NomNom membership in between. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for the breakdown.

## Why it feels different

Generic search **ranks**. Anonymous stars **hide who is accountable**. Here, every recommendation stays tied to a **real profile and post** you can open—so trust is explainable, not a black box.
