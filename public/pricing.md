# Pricing — NomNom

## Free
- Price: $0/month
- No credit card required
- Features:
  - Browse all public (free) creator lists
  - Create an account and set your taste preferences
  - Follow creators and build your discovery feed
  - Core maps and discovery experience
  - Create your own public restaurant lists

## Premium (Snapshot — one-time)
- Price: Shown in app before purchase (varies by creator)
- Type: One-time payment, permanent access to a specific list as it appears at purchase time
- Includes:
  - Full access to a specific creator list (all restaurants)
  - Permanent access — no recurring charges
  - No updates when the creator adds new places (use Subscription for updates)
- Best for: One-time trip planning or trying a single creator's picks

## Premium (Subscription — monthly)
- Price: Set by each creator, shown in app before purchase
- Type: Monthly recurring, cancel anytime
- Includes:
  - All paid lists from that creator
  - Email notifications when creator adds, removes, or updates a restaurant
  - Access continues until end of billing period after cancellation
- Best for: Regularly following a specific creator whose taste you trust

## Notes
- Pricing is per creator — each creator sets their own Subscription price
- Payment methods depend on region at checkout
- Cancel Subscriptions anytime via Settings → My Subscriptions
- Snapshots are permanent, non-refundable one-time purchases
- Contact: https://nomnom.app/contact-us
