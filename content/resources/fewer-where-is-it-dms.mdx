If people already DM you “onde é?”, you are running a restaurant search product
in your inbox. The clip dies in 24 hours. The screenshot usually crops your
name. The next follower asks again.

## Put the work on a profile

- Publish the list you already paste in private chats. [Lists](/features/lists)
  are free to keep public.
- Leave the first restaurants open. That is the magnet.
- When it is worth it, turn on a **Snapshot** (one-time, that version of the
  list) or a **Subscription** (monthly, all of your paid lists, email when you
  add a spot). Prices are yours. Payouts go through Stripe. NomNom is not a
  membership sitting between you and the diner. Details on
  [pricing](/pricing) and the [creators use case](/use-cases/creators).

## Rules that keep credit

- If it is not on the list, it did not ship.
- Bio points at the NomNom profile, not fourteen link-in-bio tiles.
- Answer the DM with the list link, not another screenshot.

You do not have to paywall anything. You do have to stop letting Stories be
the only system of record.
