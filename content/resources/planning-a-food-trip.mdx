Friends land Friday. The list needed to exist Tuesday. A long “where to eat in
Lisbon” essay is a tab they will not reopen in the Uber.

## One list, not ten screenshots

Make a NomNom list named for the visit — **friends from Porto, three nights** —
not for the city. Eight to twelve spots you would defend in person. Mark what
needs a booking. Include one tired-Sunday place and one walkable-from-the-hotel
place. Skip anywhere you would not put your own name on.

Share the **list link**. Prints in WhatsApp are how credit and hours go missing.
If they already lived in Maps, [import the public list](/resources/export-google-maps-saved-places)
and then cut it down.

## Decide like a local host

Large group? [Table](/features/table) — vote on the link, even for people
without the app. Stuck between two good places?
[NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette) on *that* list, not on a city ranking.
Lisbon visitors can also try [/roleta/lisboa](/roleta/lisboa) to see the
mechanic.
