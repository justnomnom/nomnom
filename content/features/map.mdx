The map is not a second Google Maps. It is **the pins that already belong to
your people**, on the block where you are standing.

## The loop

1. Pan to the neighbourhood you will actually be in.
2. Tap a pin. See who saved it and what they wrote.
3. Put it on a [list](/features/lists).
4. Send that shortlist to whoever is coming with you.

If the pin has no named person behind it, treat it as a rumour. Named
recommendations are the product; density of anonymous pins is not.

## With the rest of the app

Lost in the group chat after you have a shortlist? Open a
[Table](/features/table). Cannot pick between three good pins?
[NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette) spins a pool tied to people you follow, not a
generic ranking.
