Saved places in Google Maps are a serious archive and a terrible Friday plan.
They mix petrol stations, a 2019 lunch, and the one restaurant you actually
meant to try. NomNom imports the list — it does not scrape your private
account.

## What you need

- A **public** Google Maps list (share link).
- A NomNom account.

Private Maps lists cannot be read from a link. Make a copy you are willing to
share, or move the places you still want into a public list first.

## Steps

1. In Google Maps, open the list and copy the share link. Valid links look like
   `maps.app.goo.gl` or `google.com/maps`.
2. In NomNom, open **Lists** and tap **Import from Google Maps**.
3. Paste the link and tap **Find spots**.
4. NomNom shows what it already has. Check the names. Unmatched places are
   labelled **not on NomNom yet** — they will **not** be created on the fly.
   The team is told so the catalogue can grow; you are not stuck with a fake pin.
5. Confirm. You get a NomNom list you can rename, keep private, share, follow
   from, [spin NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette), or drop into a [Table](/features/table).

## After the import

Split the dump. “Lisbon” is not an occasion. Make **date night**, **late
kitchen**, **visiting friends**. Follow those lists instead of a Maps dump you
never reopen. The longer walkthrough of why short lists win is in
[Lists](/features/lists).

If a place you love did not match, it is not in the catalogue yet — not a
silent skip of a place we pretended to know.
