A 4.6 no longer tells you if *you* will like dinner. It tells you that a large,
mixed crowd did not hate it. Tourists, office lunches, one anniversary, and a
bad Tuesday all sit in the same average.

NomNom’s bet is narrower: **named people you already trust**. The
[feed](/features/feed) is those people. On a restaurant, **NomNom Circle** is
activity from the people you follow — not a global scoreboard.

## What we are not saying

We are not saying “there is no advertising”. Discover can carry **labelled**
sponsored placements. We will not sell a creator’s ranking inside their list.
If you cannot see who is speaking, it is not a recommendation.

We are not publishing a scraped “inflation index” of Lisbon scores here. Until
that dataset exists, the argument stays qualitative and product-true.

## A better default

1. Follow three palates you already believe.
2. Save into [lists](/features/lists) with an occasion in the title.
3. When the group stalls, [Table](/features/table) or
   [NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette) — still on *your* shortlist.
