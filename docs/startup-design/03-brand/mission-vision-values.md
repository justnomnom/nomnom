# Mission, Vision, Values — NomNom

**Phase:** 5 — Brand  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Note:** Extends existing `BRAND.md`; does not replace it. Options below for founder remix.

---

## Mission (pick or remix)

**Option A — Decide together**  
Help people in Portugal choose where to eat with friends — from people they trust — without forcing another app install.

**Option B — Trust over stars**  
Make restaurant picks attributable to real people again, so “onde vamos?” doesn’t default to ads, algorithms, or tourist traps.

**Option C — Save → table**  
Turn saved food content into places you can actually go tonight.

**Recommendation [Opinion]:** **A** — matches GTM (group decide / shareable lists) and research gate; B is true but broader; C is V1-shaped and platform-fragile.

---

## Vision (pick or remix)

**Option A**  
Every Lisbon group chat that asks “onde vamos comer?” has a shortlist from humans they believe — and the open table isn’t owned by booking or delivery apps.

**Option B**  
Portugal becomes the place where creator restaurant guides pay the people who curate them, not only the platforms that host the Reels.

**Option C**  
A Europe-wide habit: share a NomNom link the way people share a Maps pin — except the group can decide, not just stare.

**Recommendation [Opinion]:** **A** for Year 1 credibility; C is expansion fantasy until Lisbon density exists; B is monetisation vision, secondary.

---

## Working values (decision filters)

1. **Trust over scale** — Name the source. Label #pub. No fake “verified” without a criterion (`BRAND.md`).
2. **Link before app** — If it doesn’t work from a public list link for a stranger, it isn’t ready.
3. **Locals own the graph** — Tourists may pay; they don’t define what “good” means.
4. **Earn, don’t claim** — No “hidden gem” / “best of” without evidence.
5. **Creator dignity** — Don’t promise take-rates that VAT + store fees destroy; disclose sponsorship ruthlessly.

---

## Flags

**Yellow:** Existing brand is playful; strategy is narrower (decide/shortlist) — hero copy should shift toward group decide without losing NomNom energy.  
**Red:** Vision B without live payments is marketing ahead of product.
