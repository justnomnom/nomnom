Alfama is steep and the kitchens close. This city collection is three blocks
and one tasca, not a hill-by-hill itinerary.

Start at [Zé da Mouraria](/countries/portugal/lisbon/restaurants/ze-da-mouraria)
if you can sit by 12:30. Save the list before you lose signal in the lanes.
