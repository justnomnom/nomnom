# Go-to-Market — NomNom

**Phase:** 4 — Strategy  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Budget posture:** €0 paid acquisition for 90 days  
**Beachhead:** Arroios / Campo de Ourique / Alvalade (Lisbon residential) — not Baixa/Chiado tourist core for acquisition

---

## 1. Launch strategy

**Thesis:** Distribution is the product. Ship attention to auth-free pages; convert organisers and creators; monetise only after share loop works.

**Sequence:**
1. **Fix share + single-player** (OG cards, personal saves useful alone).
2. **Seed 30 creator `/u/handle` pages** before outreach; DM “já fiz isto.”
3. **Instrument public list shares** (opens/share, activation).
4. **Push `/roleta/lisboa`** as press/story magnet.
5. **Only then** turn on Snapshot for 1–2 flagship lists (tourist/trip) + one marked sponsorship experiment.

**Do not launch as:** “New restaurant discovery app” App Store campaign.

---

## 2. First 100 customers plan

“Customer” here = **activated organisers or creators**, not installs.

| Cohort | N | How |
|---|---|---|
| Claimed creators | 20–30 | Pre-build → DM → claim → Story once |
| Group organisers | 40–50 | Founder outreach: send real shortlists into own groups; recruit friends-of-friends |
| Repeat list makers | 20–30 | People who create a second list within 14 days |
| Paying (stretch) | 5–10 | Snapshot buyers *or* 1 restaurant sponsor — learning, not revenue |

**Week-by-week (founder hours):**
- W1–2: Share UX audit + Remotion OG; import/single-player path; creator shortlist of 150.
- W3–6: Pre-build 30 pages; send DMs; onboarding calls; track claim rate (target 15–25%).
- W5–8: Roleta pitch to 5 PT lifestyle editors; instrument referral metrics.
- W8–12: Snapshot fake-door or live SKU on 2 lists; 5 restaurant sponsor conversations.

## 4. Partnerships & ecosystem
- Creators: product trade, not cash posts (€250–€2,500/Reel unaffordable).
- Restaurants (later): marked list sponsorship without per-cover fee.
- Press: Roleta story angle only.
- Explicitly **not** Year-1: TheFork/Uber co-marketing (they are the free substitute).

---

## 5. Timeline & milestones

| Milestone | Target | Kill/pivot signal |
|---|---|---|
| Share loop live (OG + analytics) | Day 14 | If not shipped, pause creator outreach |
| 10 creators claimed | Day 45 | &lt;5 claims → messaging/product fail |
| 30 creators claimed | Day 75 | Stretch |
| ≥1.5 opens per share (trailing 50 shares) | Day 60 | &lt;1.0 → OG/UX fail |
| First € from Snapshot or sponsor | Day 90 | €0 + no interview WTP → kill consumer SKU path |
| Interviews: 8 Lisbon diners | Parallel W1–4 | Deferred was chosen — **still Experiment #1 in Phase 8** |

## Flags

**Red:** Uber already signed PT creators for free collections — exclusivity window is closing.  
**Yellow:** First 100 “customers” can be vanity if they never share; optimise for shares and claims, not signups.
