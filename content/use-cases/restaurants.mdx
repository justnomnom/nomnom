People do not pick restaurants from a spreadsheet. They pick from **who** suggested the spot — creators and locals they already trust. NomNom is **diner- and creator-first**: your win is showing up on **lists and profiles** where that trust is explicit, with **context** alongside the name.

## What this means for you

- **Intent that fits**: a table booked after a **named recommendation** often matches your concept better than a generic “near me” tap.
- **Explainable discovery**: your spot appears next to **real meals and stories** from a named person, not only an aggregate score.
- **You don’t need an account to be listed**: creators add restaurants they actually visit. Claim and update your page if you want photos, hours, and links kept current.
- **Creator lists stay editorial**: rankings inside a creator’s list reflect their taste, not your spend. Want broader reach? Sponsored placements in Discover are clearly labelled.

## How to land on more lists

The honest version: cook food creators want to write about. We can help by making sure your page is accurate and easy to add — so when a creator decides to recommend you, the listing already looks the way you’d want it to.
