A lunch list is three places you would actually walk to between meetings. Not a
city dump.

Start with [Time Out Market](/countries/portugal/lisbon/restaurants/time-out-market)
when the group cannot agree, or [Zé da Mouraria](/countries/portugal/lisbon/restaurants/ze-da-mouraria)
when you can sit down by 12:30.

Save it as a NomNom List so the next person does not reopen Maps. Import an old
Maps list if you must, then cut it: [export Google Maps saved places](/resources/export-google-maps-saved-places).
