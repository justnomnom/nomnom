The dinner did not fail at the booking. It failed at message 37. **Table** is
how the person who has to land a reservation by Wednesday stops running a
WhatsApp referendum.

## How it works

1. Pick a few spots from a list you already trust — not twelve, not fifty.
2. Open a Table and share the link.
3. People vote. Friends can vote **without installing the app**.
4. Book the winner. If nobody votes, [spin NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette) on
   that same shortlist.

## Rules that keep it humane

- Whoever organises chooses the universe. The group chooses inside it.
- Put group size, neighbourhood, and kitchen hours in the notes — the details
  generic “best of” lists skip.
- Never more than five spots on the vote.

More on the pattern: [Decide dinner without the group chat](/resources/decide-dinner-without-the-group-chat)
and the [hosts use case](/use-cases/hosts).
