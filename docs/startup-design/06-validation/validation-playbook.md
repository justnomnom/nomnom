# Validation Playbook — NomNom

**Phase:** 8 — Validation  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Rule:** Interviews were deferred in Phase 3.7 → **they are Experiment #1. No exceptions.**

---

## Experiments (cheapest first)

### 1. Lisbon diner interviews (n=8–12)
- **Tests:** Problem intensity; group shortlist / share behaviour; WTP for Snapshot at €3.99–7.99; #pub distrust; would they open a stranger’s NomNom link
- **How:** 25–30 min; protocol in skill `customer-interview.md`; personas Inês/Tiago; no pitch until end
- **Measure:** Problem confirm unprompted; workaround behaviour; pay intent (verbal ≠ card)
- **Pass:** ≥6/8 confirm decide/save pain; ≥2 show paid/workaround behaviour; ≥3 would pay *or* strongly refuse with clear reason
- **Fail:** Polite interest only; no behaviour signals
- **Time/cost:** 2 weeks / €0–50 incentives

### 2. Share-link field test (own groups)
- **Tests:** Opens/share; OG CTR; decide completion
- **How:** Send 20 real public list links into real groups; instrument
- **Measure:** Opens/share; votes; time-to-decision
- **Pass:** ≥1.5 opens/share; ≥40% of opens engage
- **Fail:** &lt;1.0 opens/share
- **Time/cost:** 1 week / €0

### 3. Creator claim rate (pre-built pages)
- **Tests:** Supply-side distribution without cash
- **How:** Pre-build 30 `/u/handle`; DM template from GTM
- **Measure:** View → claim → Story share
- **Pass:** ≥15% claim; ≥5 Story mentions
- **Fail:** &lt;5% claim
- **Time/cost:** 3–4 weeks / founder hours

### 4. Snapshot fake door
- **Tests:** Click intent to pay before Stripe live
- **How:** “Comprar lista €5” on 2 lists → waitlist / “em breve”
- **Measure:** CTR on buy; email capture
- **Pass:** ≥5% of unique viewers click buy (n≥200 views)
- **Fail:** &lt;1%
- **Time/cost:** 3 days eng / €0

### 5. Restaurant sponsor concierge
- **Tests:** B2B willingness
- **How:** 10 conversations; offer marked slot €200 for 30 days on a live neighbourhood list
- **Measure:** LOI or paid
- **Pass:** ≥1 paid or 2 firm LOIs
- **Fail:** Universal no
- **Time/cost:** 2 weeks

### 6. Roleta press pitch
- **Tests:** Earned media channel
- **How:** Pitch 5 outlets
- **Pass:** ≥1 pickup
- **Fail:** 0 — non-fatal
- **Time/cost:** 3 days

### Explicitly later / avoid
Paid Meta CAC test (economics closed unless LTV flips).  
Full Stripe+creator marketplace before experiments 1–4.
