You are the person who books. You do not need fifty “best in Lisbon” tabs. You
need **three creditable spots** that take the group, before Wednesday.

## The job

- A shortlist, not a dump — occasion in the title, constraints in the notes
  (headcount, neighbourhood, last kitchen).
- A [Table](/features/table) so people vote on a link instead of stacking
  Screenshots. They do not have to install the app.
- [NomNom Roulette](/features/roulette) only after the universe is honest. Two spins,
  then you reserve.

## Why this is not Maps

Maps will happily sort by rating and ignore that nobody can sit twelve. Your
list carries the policy. Import an old Maps list if you must, then **cut it**
([how to import](/resources/export-google-maps-saved-places)).

Walkthrough: [Decide dinner without the group chat](/resources/decide-dinner-without-the-group-chat).

**Start:** [Let’s NomNom!](/auth/register) and make the list for this
week’s table — not for the whole city.
