## Dimensions below 7 — fixes

### Team Credentials (4)
- **Weak:** No named accomplishments beyond “built the product.”  
- **Fix:** Add 2–3 verifiable bullets (prior products, creator network, community wins) *or* named advisor with food/creator distribution. State weekly hours / full-time.

### Ask Clarity (7 — borderline)
- Confirm €200k vs Bynd floor; consider €250–300k clubbed round language if targeting Bynd as lead.

### Business Model (7 — borderline)
- Bring one pricing-page or sponsor LOI experiment result into the deck when available.

## Delivery tips

- **60-second rule:** Opener + insight must land before market math.  
- **Invite conversation:** Pause after “They don’t own auth-free trusted lists.”  
- **Own the numbers:** Know BrightLocal 45%, Uberall ~83%, €28M TAM derivation at a high level.  
- **“I don’t know”:** WTP and interviews — say the experiment design, don’t bluff.  
- **Practice the 2-sentence opener** until effortless.
